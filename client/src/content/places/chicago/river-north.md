River North sits just north of the Chicago River, across from the Loop. Its streets are full of restaurants, bars and galleries, so it suits a group dinner, a birthday night out or drinks after work for people with offices on either side of the river. Its stations are a short ride from the Loop, so it also works when part of the group starts downtown.

Places that work as a meeting point:

- the Merchandise Mart, on the river at Wells Street, with an 'L' station built into it
- Marina City, the two round towers on State Street at the river
- the Grand Red Line station on State Street, at Grand Avenue
- the Chicago Brown and Purple Line station, on Chicago Avenue at Franklin Street

## Getting around

- **The Red Line** runs under State Street, with stops at [Grand](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/grre), at 521 N. State St., and [Chicago](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/chir), at 800 N. State St.
- **The Brown and Purple lines** stop at [Merchandise Mart](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=98), at 350 N. Wells St., and at [Chicago](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=37), at 300 W. Chicago Ave.
- **The #66 bus** on Chicago Avenue stops at both Chicago stations, so it links the Red Line side of the area with the Brown Line side.

At night and on weekends:

- The Red Line runs at all hours, every day, as the [Red Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/redline/) says. Grand and Chicago on State Street are the stops to use late at night.
- The Brown Line stops overnight. Its [timetable](https://www.transitchicago.com/brownline/) has its last trains leaving the Loop around 2 a.m. on weekdays and Saturdays, and earlier on Sundays.
- The Purple Line stops here only during weekday rush periods, as the [Purple Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/purpleline/) says. At other times, only the Brown Line stops at Merchandise Mart and Chicago.
