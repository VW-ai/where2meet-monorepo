Wicker Park is a neighborhood northwest of downtown, built around the six-way crossing of North, Damen and Milwaukee avenues. Milwaukee Avenue and Division Street are lined with bars, cafes, restaurants and shops, so it suits a weekend brunch, a night out with friends or a show at a music venue. It is one Blue Line ride from downtown and from O'Hare.

Places that work as a meeting point:

- the corner of North, Damen and Milwaukee avenues, under the Blue Line tracks
- Wicker Park itself, the park on Damen Avenue south of North Avenue
- the Division Blue Line station, where Division Street meets Milwaukee Avenue
- the entrance to the 606 trail on Damen Avenue, at the north edge of the area

## Getting around

- **The Blue Line** stops at [Damen](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=50), an elevated station at 1558 N. Damen Ave., and [Division](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=54), a subway station at 1200 N. Milwaukee Ave. Trains run downtown one way and toward O'Hare the other.
- **Buses** #50, #56 and #72 stop at Damen, and #9, #X9, #56 and #70 stop at Division, as the station pages list.

At night and on weekends:

- The Blue Line runs at all hours, every day, as the [Blue Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/blueline/) says. For a late plan, both stations have trains through the night.
