The Financial District is San Francisco's office core, between Market Street, Kearny Street and the waterfront. It suits plans that start at work, like a team lunch, a client coffee or drinks after the office. The Ferry Building and the Embarcadero promenade at its east end also suit a weekend walk or a meal by the bay.

Most of the district is within a short walk of two stations on Market Street, Montgomery St and Embarcadero. Both have BART and Muni Metro, so a group coming from the East Bay, the Peninsula and the west side of the city can reach either one.

Places that work as a meeting point:

- the front of the Ferry Building, facing the foot of Market Street
- Embarcadero Center, the four office towers and shops above the Embarcadero station
- the Salesforce Transit Center and its rooftop park
- the California Street cable car stop at Drumm Street

## Getting around

- **Embarcadero** and **Montgomery St** each have four BART lines. The [Embarcadero station page](https://www.bart.gov/stations/embr) says it is close to the Ferry Building, Embarcadero Center, the Salesforce Transit Center and the California Street cable car.
- The J, K, L, M and N Muni Metro lines stop at both stations. The N continues past Embarcadero along The Embarcadero to 4th and King, as the [N Judah page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/n-judah) shows.
- **The F Market & Wharves** streetcar runs along Market Street, loops past the Ferry Building and follows the waterfront to Fisherman's Wharf. It runs from 7 a.m. to midnight daily, as its [route page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/f-market-wharves) says.
- **The California cable car** runs between Embarcadero and Van Ness Avenue from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily, as its [route page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/california-cable-car) says.

Some of these change at night and on weekends:

- BART opens at 5 a.m. on weekdays, 6 a.m. on Saturdays and 8 a.m. on Sundays, and it closes around midnight, as its [schedules page](https://www.bart.gov/schedules) says.
- From 12 a.m. to 5 a.m., Muni's [Owl buses](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/routes-stops/muni-owl-service-late-night-transportation) run every half hour. The L Owl and N Owl replace those two Muni Metro lines, and the F's route page points riders to the L Owl at night.
- The 38 Geary runs 24 hours a day and ends at the Salesforce Transit Center, as its [route page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/38-geary) shows.
