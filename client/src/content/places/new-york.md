In New York, the trip decides the plan more than the venue does. A group spread across Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and New Jersey can each face a different subway line, a transfer or a PATH train. So the most convenient spot is usually one on top of a big station, where several lines stop.

These pages cover four Manhattan areas where many lines come together:

- **[Midtown](/blog/new-york/midtown)** has Times Sq-42 St, Grand Central and Herald Square. Between them, they serve 18 subway lines on weekdays, plus the 42 St Shuttle.
- **[Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan)** has Fulton St and the World Trade Center, with PATH trains to New Jersey and the ferry to Staten Island.
- **[Union Square](/blog/new-york/union-square)** has eight lines in one station at 14th Street, where Greenwich Village, the East Village, Chelsea and the Flatiron District meet.
- **[Upper West Side](/blog/new-york/upper-west-side)** is mostly homes, with the 1, 2 and 3 under Broadway and the B and C under Central Park West.

Outside Manhattan, Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr in Brooklyn has the 2, 3, 4, 5, B, D, N, Q and R. Jackson Hts-Roosevelt Av in Queens has the 7, E, F, M and R.

## Getting around

The subway runs all night, but not every line does. Late at night some lines stop and others change route. The MTA's [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) shows what runs from about 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.

- The B and W run on weekdays only. The Z runs only at weekday rush hours.
- The C runs from about 6 a.m. to 11 p.m., as its [MTA timetable](https://www.mta.info/schedules/subway/c-train) says.
- The 42 St Shuttle between Times Sq and Grand Central stops from about midnight to 6 a.m. ([MTA timetable](https://www.mta.info/schedules/subway/42-st-shuttle)).
- Track work changes service on some weekends. Look up every line in your plan on the MTA's [planned service changes](https://www.mta.info/alerts) page before you send it.

Trains and ferries from outside the subway:

- PATH trains from Newark, Jersey City and Hoboken stop at [World Trade Center](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/stations/world-trade-center-station.html). Trains from Jersey City and Hoboken also run uptown to 33 St, with stops at Christopher St, 9 St, 14 St and 23 St.
- Late at night, PATH runs only from Newark to World Trade Center and from Journal Square to 33 St by way of Hoboken. The 9 St and 23 St stations close from midnight to 5 a.m., as the [PATH schedules](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/schedules-maps.html) say.
- LIRR trains stop at Penn Station and Grand Central. Metro-North trains stop at Grand Central, and NJ Transit trains at Penn Station.
- The [Staten Island Ferry](https://siferry.com/) is free and runs 24 hours a day between St George and Whitehall Street in Lower Manhattan.
