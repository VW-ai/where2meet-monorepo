Where to take a date in San Francisco comes down to two things, the train each of you rides home and whether you booked ahead. A speakeasy near Union Square, a tiki bar in Hayes Valley and a late Italian dinner on Valencia Street all sit a short walk from BART or Muni Metro. Maybe you live in Oakland, your date lives in the Sunset, and neither of you wants to cross the city twice.

This guide gives seven places in five neighborhoods. It then covers trips from the East Bay and the Peninsula, reservations, views and the last train. It is part of our guide to [San Francisco](/blog/san-francisco).

## Where to take a date in San Francisco without a car?

Head to Union Square, the Mission or SoMa, because BART stops at Powell St, 16th St Mission and 24th St Mission, and Caltrain ends at 4th and King. Every pick below is within about a 15-minute walk of a station.

The hours, reservations, price levels and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of November 2026, with walks from the nearest station.

Two picks are in the [Mission District](/blog/san-francisco/mission-district), two are a few blocks from [Union Square](/blog/san-francisco/union-square), and two are in [SoMa](/blog/san-francisco/soma).

## What if your date lives across the bay?

Pick a place near a station on their line, so the person with the longer trip gets the shorter walk. In San Francisco that usually means a BART station for the East Bay and 4th and King for the Peninsula.

Every BART train that crosses the bay stops at Powell St, 16th St Mission and 24th St Mission. A rider from Oakland or Berkeley reaches five of these places without a transfer and walks 10 minutes or less from the train. A Peninsula rider on BART from Millbrae or Daly City reaches the same stations.

Caltrain riders arrive at the [San Francisco station at 4th and King](https://www.caltrain.com/station/sanfrancisco), where the N Judah and T Third also stop. That makes a SoMa dinner the easy choice, and the T Third runs up to Union Square for a drink after.

## Do you need to book, or can you walk in?

Book for any Friday or Saturday, except at Smuggler's Cove, which takes no reservations. Six of the seven places take bookings.

Lore is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays, and ROOH's dinner ends at 9:30 p.m. on most nights, so book it for the start of the evening and have drinks after. For a date that runs late, Bourbon & Branch and Lore stay open until 2 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

![](bourbon-and-branch-liquid-nitrogen-cocktail.jpg)

## Is a view worth planning the date around?

It can be, if you go before dark. Cityscape and The View Lounge both open at 4 p.m., so an early drink there catches the last light.

San Francisco's fog can cover the downtown towers in the evening, and then the windows show mostly cloud. Choose on the drinks and the price instead. Cityscape is moderately priced, while The View Lounge is expensive and sits closer to the train.

## Common questions

### How late can a date run if one of you takes BART home?

BART runs until about midnight every night and doesn't run overnight, as its [schedules page](https://www.bart.gov/schedules) shows, so an East Bay rider should look up the last train from their station before the date. Inside the city, Muni's [Owl buses](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/routes-stops/muni-owl-service-late-night-transportation) run every half hour from midnight to 5 a.m. Check [BART's service advisories](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/advisories) on the day of the date.

### What is a good first date in San Francisco that isn't just drinks?

An escape room or a late dinner gives you something to talk about besides each other. Lore pairs an escape room with a cocktail bar, and Bottega serves dinner until 11 p.m. or later.

### Is the Mission a good area for a date?

Yes, if one of you rides BART, since both Mission stations are on every line that crosses the bay. Valencia Street has dinner, and 16th Street has bars a short walk from the train.
