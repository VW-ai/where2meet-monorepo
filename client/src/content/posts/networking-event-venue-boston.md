A good networking event venue in Boston gives you a room or a section you can hold for a set time, a few minutes' walk from a T station. For a meetup with a speaker, that usually means a coworking event room downtown or in Back Bay, and for a mixer, a bar that takes group reservations. You run a meetup group or a professional association, the RSVPs are coming in, and guests will arrive from Cambridge, Somerville and the South Shore at 6 p.m.

This post lists seven places, then covers rooms versus bars, start times and the trip in. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which networking event venue in Boston fits your meetup?

Match the venue to the format. Pick an event room when someone speaks, a bar when people only mingle, and a cafe for a small morning group.

## Should you book an event room or a bar?

Book a room when the meetup has a program. Book a bar when the point is open conversation.

- **A room with a program.** Talks, panels and pitch nights need seats, a screen and a door you can close. Workbar says its rooms range from about 20 to 100 guests and are for professional events such as mixers and panels.
- **A bar for mingling.** A mixer works best when people can stand and move between groups. Back Bay Social, Apex and Headroom take reservations, so ask each one how many guests a held section fits.
- **Both in one night.** Hold the talk at Workbar Back Bay, then walk the group to The Irving or Back Bay Social on Boylston Street for drinks.

## What time should a networking meetup start?

Start at 6 or 6:30 p.m. on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday. That gives guests time to leave work and still catch a train home.

Workbar takes weekday events only after 5 p.m., so 6 p.m. is the earliest realistic start there. Apex is closed on Monday and Headroom is closed on Sunday, so check the day before you post the invite.

A breakfast meetup avoids evening plans entirely. Sip Café opens at 7 a.m. on most weekdays, which leaves an hour before most offices start.

## How will guests get there from across Boston?

Choose a venue near a transfer station so guests change trains at most once.

- **Downtown.** Workbar Downtown, Apex and Sip Café are near State, which has the Orange and Blue lines. Park Street and Downtown Crossing are also within a short walk.
- **Back Bay.** Workbar Back Bay is at Arlington, and The Irving is at Copley. Both stations are on every Green Line branch.
- **Fort Point.** Headroom is next to Courthouse. Take the Red Line to South Station, then a Silver Line bus one stop.

## Common questions

### How much does a networking event venue cost in Boston?

Workbar's event space starts at $250 an hour, with prices that change by location and day. Bars with a reserved section set their own terms, so ask each one about a minimum spend.

### Which of these places can host a weekend networking event?

Workbar takes events anytime on weekends. Apex opens at 1 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday, and Back Bay Social is open every day from 11 a.m.

### How late can a networking event run if guests take the T?

The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday. Ending by 9 p.m. leaves plenty of room for guests with long trips.
