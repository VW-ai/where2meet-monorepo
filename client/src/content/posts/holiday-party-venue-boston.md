The best holiday party venue in Boston for most groups is a place that books tables for a crowd and sits a short walk from a T station. For an office party, that often means the Seaport, where three game bars and a dinner spot sit within a 10-minute walk of Courthouse station. You have a head count, a budget and a date in December, and half the guests leave from a desk downtown while the rest come in from Cambridge or the South Shore.

This post lists eight places, then covers games versus dinner, when to book, and how guests get there and home. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which holiday party venue in Boston suits your group?

Pick the area first, then the format. Four picks cluster in the Seaport, two are downtown, and two serve guests who live in Charlestown or South Boston.

## Should an office holiday party venue have games or just dinner?

Choose games when coworkers don't all know each other. Choose dinner when the group is small enough to talk across one table.

A game gives people from different teams something to do together in the first hour. Flight Club and Kings are both at 60 Seaport Boulevard, so a group can book one and move to the other if the night runs long. SPIN is in Fort Point, about a 10-minute walk from Courthouse.

For a dinner, ask each restaurant whether it has a private room and what the minimum spend is. If the group is bigger than ten, see our tips on a [restaurant for a big group in Boston](/blog/boston/restaurant-big-group-boston).

## When should you book a December party?

Book in October or early November. Other teams want the same December Fridays, so hold a Wednesday or Thursday if your date can move.

Check closing days before you pick a date. Central Wharf Co. is closed Sunday to Tuesday, and SPIN is closed Sunday and Monday. A Thursday works at all eight picks.

A lunch party avoids the evening rush. Carrie Nation opens at 11 a.m. on weekdays, and Flight Club opens at noon.

## How do guests get there and home?

Start from the station most guests pass through. For the Seaport picks, that is South Station.

- **To the Seaport.** The Seaport has no subway station. Take the Red Line to South Station, then any [Silver Line SL1, SL2 or SL3 bus](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/741/line) one stop to Courthouse.
- **To downtown.** Central Wharf Co. is near Aquarium on the Blue Line. Carrie Nation is near Park Street on the Red and Green lines.
- **To Charlestown and South Boston.** Prima is a walk from the Orange Line, and Capo is a walk from the Red Line at Broadway.

![](courthouse-station-silver-line-bus-boston.jpg)

Plan the trip home before the last toast. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or by 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

## Common questions

### What is a good office holiday party venue in Boston?

For a mixed team, a game bar such as Flight Club, Kings or SPIN gives people something to do together. For a smaller team that wants to talk, a reserved table at Central Wharf Co. or Empire works.

### How early should you book a holiday party in Boston?

Book eight weeks ahead if you want a Friday in December. Weeknights and lunch slots leave you more choice closer to the date.

### Which picks are open on a Sunday?

Flight Club, Kings, Empire, Prima and Capo open on Sundays, and Carrie Nation opens until 5 p.m. SPIN and Central Wharf Co. are closed.
