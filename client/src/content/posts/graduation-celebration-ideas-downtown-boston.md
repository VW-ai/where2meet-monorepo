The best graduation celebration ideas in Downtown Boston start from one fact, the hour the ceremony ends. A morning ceremony leaves time for a weekend brunch or a long lunch, and an afternoon one points to a booked dinner near South Station. You have grandparents coming in on the commuter rail, a graduate who wants to see friends, and parents who want one meal with everyone at the table.

This post suggests eight places, then covers which meal to book on which day, how relatives get downtown and where the graduate's friends can go after dinner. It's part of our guide to [Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown).

## Which graduation celebration ideas fit Downtown Boston?

Pick one meal for the family and one later stop for the graduate. All eight places below take reservations, and they run from the Faneuil Hall markets to Chinatown station.

## Should you book brunch, lunch or dinner after the ceremony?

Book for the hour the family can actually sit down, about two hours after the ceremony ends. Gowns go back, photos take time, and a crowd of families leaves slowly.

The day of the week narrows the list more than the hour does.

- **Saturday.** Every place on the list is open. Book brunch or lunch near Faneuil Hall, or dinner at Trade or Blu.
- **Sunday.** Trade and Blu are closed. A Sunday dinner falls to The Palm, 1928 Rowes Wharf, Yvonne's or Fete.
- **Monday or Tuesday.** Fete is closed, and Trade is closed on Monday. Grana and The Palm both serve lunch on weekdays.

## How do relatives get downtown on graduation day?

Send each guest to the station closest to the meal, not the one closest to the campus. Downtown's stations sit a few blocks apart, so most guests need one change at most.

- **By commuter rail.** Trains from the south and west end at [South Station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-sstat). Trade and The Palm are both within about 8 minutes on foot.
- **From Logan Airport.** The Silver Line SL1 runs from the terminals to South Station. The Blue Line runs from Airport station to [Aquarium](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-aqucl), about 5 minutes on foot from 1928 Rowes Wharf.
- **From Cambridge.** The Red Line stops at Downtown Crossing and South Station on the way in.

For grandparents who walk slowly, compare the walk from the platform, not only the ride. Blu and Yvonne's are each about 2 minutes from a station.

## Where can the graduate's friends keep going after dinner?

Send them to a table that stays open late, then check the last train. Yvonne's and Fete both serve until 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

The subway closes overnight. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a transfer station such as Downtown Crossing by 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, or by 12:35 a.m. on other nights. Friends who stay until close need a ride share.

## Common questions

### What are graduation celebration ideas for a Sunday in Downtown Boston?

Plan a late lunch at Fete or 1928 Rowes Wharf, both open from 11 a.m. on Sunday. For dinner, The Palm opens at 4 p.m. and Yvonne's at 5 p.m.

### How far ahead should you book a graduation table downtown?

Book as soon as the ceremony date and time are announced. Other families from the same class want a table at the same hour.

### Which downtown places are moderately priced?

Grana, Sugar Factory and Blu are moderately priced. Trade and Yvonne's are expensive, and 1928 Rowes Wharf is very expensive.
