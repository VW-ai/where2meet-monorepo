If you search indoor basketball courts near me free from Back Bay, the answer is the city's BCYF community centers, which charge no fee to join. The closest, BCYF Blackstone in the South End, is about a 13-minute walk from Back Bay station. You work in the Copley or Prudential towers, the park courts are cold or under snow, and nobody in the group wants a club membership just to run a few games.

This post lists four free city gyms and two YMCAs with sliding-scale fees, all on or near the Orange Line from Back Bay. Then it covers what free really means, which gym is open on the day you can play, and which stop to ride to. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay).

## Where are the free indoor basketball courts near Back Bay?

Inside city community centers and YMCAs just south and east of Back Bay. Back Bay station is on the Orange Line, and every pick is one to four stops away or a walk from it.

The hours on these cards come from Google Maps and the City of Boston's center pages as of November 2026, and walk times are Google Maps walking directions. The fee and gym details come from the City of Boston and the YMCA of Greater Boston.

## Are these indoor basketball courts near me free to use?

The four BCYF centers are. The two YMCAs charge for membership, but on a sliding scale.

Boston Centers for Youth and Families (BCYF) [doesn't charge a fee](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-mattahunt) to join any of its centers. Each player still creates an account in the BCYF registration system before the first visit, so send that step with the invite. Each gym also hosts youth programs, so adult open gym is only part of the day. Call the center before you pick a time.

![](ymca-huntington-avenue-building-boston.jpg)

The YMCA of Greater Boston [says it won't turn anyone away](https://ymcaboston.org/membership/) for being unable to pay full fees. Its ACCESS program lowers the price based on need. A player without a membership can request a day pass from the same page.

## Which gym is open on the day your group can play?

Pick the day first, because each center keeps its own hours. Weeknights work almost everywhere, Saturday narrows the list, and Sunday leaves one free gym.

- **Weeknights after work.** All four city centers stay open until 9 p.m. The Huntington Avenue Y runs latest.
- **Saturday.** Quincy, Tobin and Shelburne open during the day. Blackstone is closed on weekends.
- **Sunday.** Shelburne is the only free option. Both YMCAs open on Sundays for members.

The city says center hours may change during school vacations. Check the page for [Blackstone](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-blackstone), [Quincy](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-quincy), [Tobin](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-tobin) or [Shelburne](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-shelburne) the week you play. The Y posts each branch's court times on its [gym schedules page](https://ymcaboston.org/healthy-living/gym-schedules/).

## Which Orange Line stop gets you there from Back Bay?

Ride one stop north for Chinatown, one stop south for Huntington Avenue, and three or four stops south for Mission Hill and Roxbury.

![](tufts-medical-center-orange-line-platforms-chinatown.jpg)

- **Tufts Medical Center**, one stop north, for the Wang YMCA and BCYF Quincy.
- **Massachusetts Avenue**, one stop south, for the Huntington Avenue YMCA. Green Line riders can take the E branch to Symphony instead.
- **Roxbury Crossing**, three stops south, for BCYF Tobin.
- **Jackson Square**, four stops south, for BCYF Shelburne.

Blackstone is the one pick most of the group can walk to straight from Back Bay station.

![](roxbury-crossing-orange-line-platform-train.jpg)

The [MBTA's Orange Line page](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/Orange/line) lists every stop. Check the [subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) the day before the game.

## Common questions

### Is the Blackstone gym really free?

Yes. BCYF says it doesn't charge a fee to join its centers. You need an account in its registration system, and adult gym hours vary, so call first.

### Can you play at a YMCA without a membership?

Ask for a day pass. The Wang Y lists its Saturday pickup run as a member program, so check guest access before you bring the group.
