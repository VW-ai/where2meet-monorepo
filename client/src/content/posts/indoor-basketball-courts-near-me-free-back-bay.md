If you search indoor basketball courts near me free from Back Bay, the answer is the city gym at the Blackstone Community Center in the South End. It's about a 13-minute walk from Back Bay station, and two free outdoor courts sit even closer while the weather holds. Your group works in the Back Bay towers, lives along the Green and Orange lines, and wants a game that doesn't cost a gym membership.

This post lists four places to play and three places to eat after. Then it covers the free indoor option, which court suits your turnout, and the ride home. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay).

## Where can a Back Bay group play pickup basketball?

Just south of Back Bay, in the South End. Every court here is within a 20-minute walk of Back Bay station, and the restaurants for after the game are on Boylston Street and Huntington Avenue.

## Are there indoor basketball courts near me free to use in Back Bay?

Not inside Back Bay itself. The closest free gym is BCYF Blackstone, a city community center on West Brookline Street.

The Boston Centers for Youth and Families (BCYF) [doesn't charge a fee](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-mattahunt) to join its centers. Each player still creates an account in its registration system before the first visit. [BCYF Blackstone](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-blackstone) lists a gymnasium and is open on weekdays only. Adult gym time changes by season, so the organizer should call before inviting anyone.

If the group can pay, the Wang YMCA runs pickup basketball on Saturday mornings, as its [November 2026 gym schedule](https://ymcaboston.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Wang-Gym-Schedule-November-2026.pdf) shows. Courts there can close for programs and rentals, so check the schedule the week you play.

## Which court suits the size of your group?

Count your players first. Titus Sparrow Park has one court, so it suits 10 for a full game or up to 8 for two half-court games.

- **Ten or fewer.** Play at Titus Sparrow Park. It's the shortest walk from Prudential and Massachusetts Avenue stations.
- **Twelve or more.** Go to Peters Park, where two courts let a second game start while the first finishes.
- **A full court at Titus Sparrow.** Walk to Peters Park, about 19 minutes away, or check whether the Blackstone gym is open, about 12 minutes away.

![](peters-park-lawn-paths-south-end-boston.jpg)

Both parks can host an evening game. The city's [Titus Sparrow Park project](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/project/titus-sparrow-park-improvements) added updated courts and new lighting in 2024, and [Boston Magazine](https://www.bostonmagazine.com/?p=561681) notes the lights at Peters Park.

## Where should the group eat after the game?

Head back toward the Prudential Center. Yard House is the shortest walk from Titus Sparrow Park, and the two Boylston Street picks take bookings.

Pick the restaurant before tip-off, not after. Then one player can book for the real headcount while the game is still on. If the group mostly wants to watch a game on TV, see our [watch party bar picks in Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay/watch-party-bar-back-bay).

## How does everyone get home after a late game?

The parks close at 11:30 p.m., and the T is still running then. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) lists typical service until about 1 a.m. on weeknights and 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

![](prudential-green-line-platform-back-bay.jpg)

Green Line riders walk to [Prudential](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-prmnl), which only the E branch serves. Orange Line riders use Massachusetts Avenue or [Back Bay](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta), which also has commuter rail.

## Common questions

### Is the Blackstone gym really free?

BCYF says it doesn't charge a fee to join its centers. You need an account in the BCYF registration system, and adult gym hours vary, so call the center first.

### Can you play at the Wang YMCA without a membership?

The pickup run is listed as a member program. The Y has a day pass request on its site, so ask about guest access before you bring the group.
