A weekend hangout on the Upper West Side goes best near the 1, 2, 3 or C, because the B doesn't run on weekends. Pick the activity first, then a spot a short walk from the line most of the group rides. Maybe two friends live uptown, one rides the 2 from Brooklyn and another crosses Central Park by bus from the Upper East Side.

This post lists eight places for a Saturday or Sunday, then covers which trains run. It is part of our guide to the [Upper West Side](/blog/new-york/upper-west-side).

## Which trains run on the Upper West Side on weekends?

Plan around the 1, 2, 3 and C. On weekends the B doesn't run, so during the day only the C stops on Central Park West.

On Broadway, the 1 stops at every station from 66 St-Lincoln Center to Cathedral Pkwy (110 St), and the 2 and 3 stop only at 72 St and 96 St. Check the MTA's [planned service changes](https://www.mta.info/alerts) before Saturday.

## Common questions

### Does the B train run on weekends on the Upper West Side?

No. The B runs on weekdays only, so on weekend days only the C stops on Central Park West.

### What can a group do on the Upper West Side when it rains?

Go to the American Museum of Natural History. It closes at 5:30 p.m., so start there early.

### How do friends from the Upper East Side get across?

By crosstown bus through Central Park. The M66, M72, M79-SBS, M86-SBS, M96 and M106 all cross to the west side, and the MTA's [schedules page](https://www.mta.info/schedules) has their timetables.
