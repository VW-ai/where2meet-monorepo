Places for a team meeting in River North start at the Merchandise Mart, where a bookable conference room and a restaurant share a floor with an 'L' platform. For a smaller group or an early start, the places for a team meeting spread east toward State Street and the Red Line. Your team works on both sides of the river. Some ride the Brown Line, some walk over from the Loop, and you have to find a room that suits every trip.

This post lists seven places, then covers 'L' stops, rooms versus tables, and weekends. It's part of our guide to [River North](/blog/chicago/river-north), in [Chicago](/blog/chicago).

## Which places for a team meeting in River North fit your group?

Pick by what the meeting needs. A session with slides needs a room with a screen, a working lunch needs a reservation, and a quick check-in needs only coffee.

## Which 'L' stop should the team aim for?

Count who rides which line before you choose. The Brown and Purple lines stop at the Mart on the west side of River North, and the Red Line runs under State Street on the east side.

- **Brown Line riders** get off at [Merchandise Mart](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=98) for the Mart and Wolf Point, or one stop north at Chicago for West Erie Street and North LaSalle Drive.
- **Red Line riders** get off at [Grand](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/grre). The Langham on Wabash is the closest pick.
- **Loop office workers** can walk across the river. From Clark/Lake, the Mart is about a 9-minute walk.
- **Metra riders** walk about 15 minutes from Ogilvie to the Mart, or about 21 from Union Station.

The Purple Line stops at the Mart only on weekday rush trips, as the [Purple Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/purpleline/) says, so a midday meeting can't count on it. Check the [CTA alerts page](https://www.transitchicago.com/alerts/) the day before.

## Should you book a room or a table?

Book a room when the meeting has an agenda and a screen. Book a table when the point is to talk, or to eat once the work is done.

WorkLife and Workbox both close at 5 p.m., so a session there has to end by late afternoon. WorkLife posts its room rates online and takes bookings through an inquiry form, so send the headcount and date early. Because its catering comes from Marshall's Landing, a morning in the room can turn into lunch on the same floor.

A restaurant suits a meeting that is mostly talk. The coffee shops take no reservations, so keep them for two or three people.

## What's open for a weekend team meeting?

Fewer places. The two Mart venues and Workbox are closed on Saturday and Sunday, so a weekend meeting moves to a restaurant or a cafe.

Travelle and Blue Bottle keep the same hours every day. On weekends avec opens at 10 a.m., an hour earlier than on weekdays, and closes at 2 p.m. before its evening hours. The Red Line runs at all hours, as its [line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/redline/) says, but the Purple Line doesn't stop at the Mart on weekends.

## Common questions

### Is there a meeting room inside the Merchandise Mart?

Yes. WorkLife Meetings rents conference rooms in Suite 250 on the second floor, between the Kinzie and Orleans entrances. Guests check in at its concierge desk.

### Is River North close to the Loop for a team meeting?

Yes, it's just across the Chicago River from the Loop. Walking from Clark/Lake to the Mart takes about 9 minutes, so a Loop team can skip the train.

### Where can two or three people meet in River North without booking?

Walk in at one of the two coffee shops on the cards. Neither takes reservations, so book a restaurant table instead once the group grows past three.
