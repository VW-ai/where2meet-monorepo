The most convenient date night bars in Manhattan are a few minutes' walk from a station on both of your lines. Pick the area by where each of you starts, then the bar by how long you want the night to last. Maybe one of you leaves work downtown, the other starts uptown, and you both need a train home.

This post suggests eight bars in Midtown, Lower Manhattan, the West Village and the Upper West Side. Then it covers booking, late trains and planning it together. It is part of our guide to [New York](/blog/new-york).

## Which date night bars in Manhattan are easy for both of you to reach?

Each of these eight bars is within a 6-minute walk of a subway entrance. Pick one on a line you can both ride without changing trains.

[Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan) works when one of you comes from Brooklyn or New Jersey. Trains from Brooklyn stop at Fulton St, and PATH trains from New Jersey stop at [World Trade Center](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/stations/world-trade-center-station.html). The [Upper West Side](/blog/new-york/upper-west-side) suits a date near home, with the 1 under Broadway from Columbus Circle to 110th Street.

## Should you book a table or walk in?

Book the bar you're crossing town for, and walk in where a short wait won't spoil the plan.

Google lists every bar here except the Rum House as taking reservations, but the St. Regis dining page offers no booking for the King Cole Bar. Book the others on their sites or by phone.

Dizzy's Club needs a ticket and lists its [set times on its own page](https://jazz.org/dizzys/). Arrive before the set starts. A set also gives a first date a fixed length. When it ends, either of you can call it a night or suggest a second stop.

![](jazz-at-lincoln-center-entrance-west-60th-street.jpg)

Check the dress code too. The St. Regis lists smart casual for the King Cole Bar on its [dining page](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/nycxr-the-st-regis-new-york/dining/).

## How late can you stay out and still get home?

Check the closing time and the late-night trains together. Some of these bars close at 10 or 11 p.m., and some lines don't run late at night.

When the first bar closes early, plan a second stop. In Midtown, the King Cole Bar closes at 11 p.m., and the Rum House stays open later.

Late at night, fewer trains stop near these bars:

- The 1 runs all night, so Employees Only, Dizzy's Club, Bin 71 and Smoke all keep a train home.
- Near Overstory, the Wall St station on the 2 and 3 has only the 2. At Fulton St, near the Beekman, the 2, 4, A and J keep running.
- At 49 St, near the Rum House, the N and Q stop, but the R and W don't.
- At 5 Av/53 St, near the King Cole Bar, only the E stops.

![](70-pine-street-crown-dusk-financial-district.jpg)

The MTA's [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) shows every line that runs overnight, and its [service changes](https://www.mta.info/alerts) page shows weekend track work.

## Common questions

### Is a bar a good first date spot?

It is when the trip is easy for both of you. One drink can end the night or turn into dinner.

### Where should we meet if one of us lives in Brooklyn and the other in New Jersey?

Try Lower Manhattan. PATH trains from New Jersey stop at World Trade Center, and the 2, 3, 4, A, C and J at Fulton St run to Brooklyn. The 2, 4, A and J also run all night.

### Which corner should we meet at?

Name a corner, not just a station. Fulton St and 59 St-Columbus Circle each have many entrances, so say which street you'll come up on, or meet at the bar's door.
