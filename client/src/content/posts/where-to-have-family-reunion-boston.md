If you're deciding where to have a family reunion in Boston, book one room near a T station for the meal and add one daytime place that both kids and grandparents enjoy. The Seaport and Back Bay each have both, a few minutes on foot from an accessible station. Your aunt is flying into Logan, your cousins are driving in from Worcester, and your grandparents need a short walk from the train.

This post lists seven places, then covers where relatives should stay, how they get in from the airport and the highways, and how to plan a day for every age. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Where to have a family reunion in Boston?

Pick a private event room or a hotel for the meal, then a park, museum or view for the afternoon. The seven below sit within about a 10-minute walk of a station.

## Where should out-of-town relatives stay?

Put relatives in a hotel within a short walk of the reunion, so nobody drives after dinner. Back Bay makes this easy.

Commuter rail trains from the south and west stop at Back Bay station, close to the Copley Plaza hotel. The Saunders Castle is about an 8-minute walk from the hotel, and View Boston about 9 minutes. That puts a hotel, a dinner room and an evening view in one walkable area.

## How do relatives get there from Logan and the highways?

Fliers ride the Silver Line or the Blue Line from the airport. Drivers park once and take the T in.

- **The Silver Line.** The SL1 bus runs from the Logan terminals to World Trade Center and South Station. The Lawn on D and the Tea Party ships are each a short walk from those stops.
- **The Blue Line.** A shuttle bus links the terminals to Airport station. The Blue Line runs from there to State, about a 2-minute walk from the State Room.
- **Driving.** Ask relatives to park at an outer station and ride in, rather than drive downtown. The MBTA lists its lots on its [parking page](https://www.mbta.com/parking).

The MBTA's [Logan Airport guide](https://www.mbta.com/destinations/logan-airport) has the current routes. Check its [subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) the day before, since weekend track work can replace trains with buses.

## How do you plan a day that works for every age?

Pair one daytime place with the meal, and keep the walk between them short. Finish while the youngest and oldest still have energy.

Here are two example days, with walk times from Google Maps:

1. **Seaport.** Tour the Tea Party ships at 10 a.m., then walk about 20 minutes to the Lawn on D for an afternoon on the grass. Grandparents can take a short taxi ride for that leg instead.
2. **Back Bay and the Public Garden.** Go up View Boston in the afternoon, then have dinner at Hampshire House. The walk between them is about 23 minutes, so ride the Green Line one stop from Copley to Arlington instead.

## Common questions

### What is a good family reunion venue in Boston for a big group?

The State Room, the Saunders Castle and Hampshire House all host private events. Contact each one for its capacity and open dates. The Copley Plaza hotel adds rooms in the same building.

### Where can a family reunion with young kids go in Boston?

The Lawn on D gives kids room to play, and the Tea Party ships and View Boston work for every age. All three are open on weekends.

### What should you book first?

Book the event room first, because its free dates set the reunion date. Then send the date to relatives so they can book flights and hotel rooms.
