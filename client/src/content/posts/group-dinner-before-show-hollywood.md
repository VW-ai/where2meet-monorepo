A group dinner before a show in Hollywood works best at a table within about 10 minutes' walk of the theatre, booked well before curtain. Choose the restaurant by the stage on your tickets, because the Pantages sits at Hollywood/Vine, the Dolby Theatre at Hollywood/Highland and the Hollywood Bowl up Highland Avenue. You have seats for eight, friends coming from the Valley, Koreatown and the Westside, and one reservation to make for all of them.

This post lists seven restaurants, then covers when to sit down, which B Line station to ride to, and where to eat if the group would rather have dinner after the show. It's part of our guide to [Hollywood](/blog/los-angeles/hollywood), in [Los Angeles](/blog/los-angeles).

## Where can you book a group dinner before a show in Hollywood?

Book on the same stretch of Hollywood Boulevard as the theatre. The Pantages and the Dolby Theatre are about a 20-minute walk apart, so a table near the wrong one adds that walk before curtain.

## How early should the group sit down?

Sit down about two hours before curtain. A big table takes longer to order, eat and pay, and the walk comes on top.

- **Check the closing time.** Fabiolus Cucina closes at 9 p.m. and Mother Wolf by 10 p.m., so both work before a show and not after it.
- **Check the first seating.** Mother Wolf opens at 5:30 p.m. on weekdays. That suits an 8 p.m. curtain at the Pantages, about 11 minutes away on foot.

For example, for a 7:30 p.m. show at the Pantages, a 5:30 p.m. table at Fabiolus Cucina leaves about an hour and a half to eat, then an 8-minute walk.

## Which B Line station should everyone ride to?

Ride to the station named for the theatre's cross street. Hollywood/Highland serves the Dolby Theatre and the Hollywood Bowl, and Hollywood/Vine serves the Pantages.

- **Dolby Theatre.** Get off at Hollywood/Highland. The theatre and La Popular share the Ovation Hollywood complex above the station.
- **Pantages.** Get off at Hollywood/Vine, a short walk from the theatre. Fabiolus Cucina, Te'Kila and Mother Wolf are each an 8- to 11-minute walk from the Pantages.
- **Hollywood Bowl.** Get off at Hollywood/Highland and eat near the station before walking up Highland Avenue. The [Hollywood Bowl's site](https://www.hollywoodbowl.com/) lists other ways to get there.
- **Drivers.** Friends from the Valley can park at Universal City or North Hollywood and ride one or two stops in, as the [Hollywood page](/blog/los-angeles/hollywood) explains.

Check [Metro's alerts](https://alerts.metro.net/) the day before the show.

## What if the group eats after the show instead?

Pick a place that stays open past midnight. A show that lets out at 10:30 p.m. rules out the early dining rooms here.

Te'Kila, Birds and El Floridita all stay open past midnight on Friday and Saturday. Watch the trains home, because the last B Line trains leave Hollywood/Highland a little after midnight. Check [Metro's schedules](https://www.metro.net/riding/schedules/) and plan a ride for anyone who stays later.

## Common questions

### How far is the Pantages from the Dolby Theatre?

It's about a 20-minute walk along Hollywood Boulevard, or one stop on the B Line between Hollywood/Vine and Hollywood/Highland. Book dinner near the theatre on your tickets.

### Do Hollywood restaurants take reservations for big groups?

Six of the seven places here take reservations. Mel's Drive-In doesn't, so it suits a group that can wait for a table. For a large party, call to confirm one table.

### Where can a group eat near the Hollywood Bowl?

La Popular and Mel's Drive-In are by the Hollywood/Highland station, about a 20-minute walk from the Bowl. Eat there, then walk up Highland Avenue together.
