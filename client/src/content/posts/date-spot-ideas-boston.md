A good date spot in Boston is a short walk from a T station on both of your lines. Downtown, Back Bay and the North End all have one within ten minutes, so pick the area first and the place second. Maybe one of you works in Cambridge, the other lives in Dorchester, and neither wants to change trains twice after dinner.

This post suggests eight places, from dinner to rooftop drinks to ping-pong, each within a 10-minute walk of a station. Then it covers booking, the last trains and planning it together. It is part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which date spot in Boston is easy for both of you to reach?

Start from the station where your two lines meet. Four downtown stations connect two lines each, and every pick below is near one of them or a station a single ride away.

:::places

The hours, reservations, price levels and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of October 2026. Walk times start at each MBTA station.

[Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown) works when you ride different lines, because Park Street has the Red and Green lines and Downtown Crossing has the Red and Orange. [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay) suits a date after work in the office towers along Boylston Street, with the Green Line under Boylston and the Orange Line at Back Bay station.

![](newbury-boston-hotel-arlington-street.jpg)

The North End is about an 8-minute walk from Haymarket, and its streets are narrow and winding. Say which corner you'll meet at, such as the Paul Revere House side of North Square.

## Should you book ahead or walk in?

Book the dinner, and keep the drink flexible. Every place here takes reservations, so the question is which part of the night can't slip.

A dinner reservation fixes the start of the date. Mamma Maria and Contessa both close by 11 p.m. or earlier, so a late table there leaves little time. A bar works as the second stop. If the first drink goes well, you can stay. If not, the night ends early without a canceled booking.

SPIN lists [20 ping-pong tables and a full bar](https://wearespin.com/location/boston/) on its own page. Book a table there on Friday and Saturday nights, the only nights it stays open past 11 p.m.

## How late can you stay out and still catch the T?

Plan to leave by about 12:30 a.m. Sunday to Thursday and 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Several of these places stay open later than the trains.

The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, to make the last connections. The subway doesn't run all night.

![](fort-point-channel-skyline-summer-street-bridge.jpg)

Commuter rail runs on its own timetable. If one of you rides it home from South Station or Back Bay, check the last train on the [MBTA schedules page](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/commuter-rail) before you choose between OFFSUIT, SPIN and a later bar.

## Common questions

### What is a good first date spot in Boston?

Choose a bar near a station on both of your lines, so either of you can leave after one drink. A rooftop bar near State or a cocktail bar near South Station keeps the trip home short.

### Where should we meet if one of us lives in Cambridge?

Look at Downtown Crossing or Park Street. The Red Line runs there from Harvard and Kendall without a transfer, and the Orange and Green lines stop there too.

### Is the North End good for a date night?

It is for an early dinner. Mamma Maria closes by 9:30 p.m. every night, so plan a later drink downtown, a short walk or one Orange Line stop away.
