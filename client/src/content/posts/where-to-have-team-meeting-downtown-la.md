Where to have a team meeting in Downtown LA depends on how the team gets there. Pick a place near the station most of the team rides to, then match it to the kind of meeting. Your team is spread across the county. Two people ride Metrolink in from Orange County or the Valley, a few take the B or D Line, and someone always drives.

This post lists six places, then covers which station to plan around, rooms versus tables, and how late the trains run. It's part of our guide to [Downtown LA](/blog/los-angeles/downtown), in [Los Angeles](/blog/los-angeles).

## Where can a team meet in Downtown LA?

Start with the room, then add the food. A meeting with slides needs a bookable room, a working lunch needs a reservation, and a quick check-in needs only coffee.

## Which station should the team plan around?

Count who comes in on which train. Metrolink and Amtrak riders all arrive at Union Station, while most Metro riders can reach 7th St/Metro Center without a transfer.

- **Metrolink riders** arrive at Union Station on the Antelope Valley, Ventura County, San Bernardino, Riverside, Orange County and 91 lines. Traxx is inside the station and Philippe is a short walk north.
- **Metro riders** on the A, B, D or E Line can all reach 7th St/Metro Center, which puts the Sheraton Grand a minute away.
- **A and E Line riders** can also stop at Little Tokyo/Arts District for CENTRL Office, or at Historic Broadway for BADMAASH.
- **Drivers** do better at Union Station, because Metro lists [parking](https://www.metro.net/riding/parking/) there and not at 7th St/Metro Center.

When most of the team comes by Metrolink, hold the meeting near Union Station and let the Metro riders make the short trip. Check [Metro's alerts](https://alerts.metro.net/) the day before.

## Do you need a meeting room or a restaurant table?

Book a room when there's an agenda and a screen. Book a table when the meeting is mostly talk, or when lunch is the reward at the end.

An hourly room keeps a two-hour planning session from costing a full day. It closes at 5 p.m. and is shut on weekends, so a Saturday meeting moves to the Sheraton Grand or a restaurant. The hotel suits a bigger group or a full day, because one inquiry can cover the room, the AV and the meals.

Restaurants work for smaller teams. Traxx and BADMAASH both take reservations, so book a table once the group is past four.

## How late can Metrolink riders stay?

Not as late as Metro riders. Metrolink runs on fixed timetables for each line, so the last train home sets the end time for anyone who rides it.

Before you fix the end time, check each rider's line on [Metrolink's schedules](https://metrolinktrains.com/schedules/). The Metro lines run later, and the [Downtown LA page](/blog/los-angeles/downtown) lists each line's last train out.

## Common questions

### Can you rent a meeting room by the hour in Downtown LA?

Yes. CENTRL Office in Little Tokyo rents rooms for 4 to 20 people by the hour or the day, on weekdays from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Each room has a TV and a whiteboard.

### Is there a restaurant inside Union Station for a group?

Yes. Traxx is inside the station near the Alameda Street entrance and takes reservations Monday through Saturday. On Sundays it serves walk-ins in the bar area.

### Where can a team meet early in the morning downtown?

Philippe The Original opens at 6 a.m. every day, and Lontra Coffee on Bunker Hill opens at 7 a.m. on weekdays. Neither takes reservations, so keep an early meeting small.
