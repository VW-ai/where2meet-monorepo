The best things to do with friends in Back Bay are a round of indoor mini golf, a weekend brunch on Newbury Street and a game at a Boylston Street sports bar. Most of them are a few minutes apart on foot, between the Copley and Hynes stations. You have a free Saturday, four friends said yes, and nobody has named a time or a place.

This post suggests seven places, then covers how to link them, which ones take bookings, where to meet and what changes on Sunday. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay). For general advice, see [how to plan a weekend hangout with friends](/blog/new-york/upper-west-side/how-to-plan-a-weekend-hangout-with-friends). For picks across the city, see [where to hang out with friends in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-hang-out-with-friends-boston).

## What are the best things to do with friends in Back Bay?

Pick one activity and one meal, then choose by your start time.

## How do you fill a whole afternoon without a long walk?

Stay on Boylston Street between Copley and Hynes, where most picks are within a 5-minute walk of the next. Here is an example for Saturday or Sunday:

1. **11 a.m.** Have coffee at Map Room Lounge.
2. **12:30 p.m.** Walk about 5 minutes to Swingers Back Bay for mini golf.
3. **3 p.m.** Walk about 3 minutes to Back Bay Social for a meal.
4. **5 p.m.** Walk about a minute to A.T. O'Keeffe's if a game is on.

The Street Bar is the exception. It's about 11 minutes on foot from Map Room Lounge, so make it the first or last stop.

On a dry day, add a stop that costs nothing. The Prudential Center's South Garden has a lawn, benches and a fountain.

## Which picks take a booking, and which are walk-ins?

Book the golf and any meal for more than a few people. Keep the walk-in places as backups.

- **Golf.** Swingers sells tickets [on its site](https://www.swingers.club/us/locations/boston). [Boston.com reported](https://www.boston.com/culture/winter-fun/2026/01/27/swingers-back-bay-mini-golf/) in January 2026 that $25 per person covers a 9-hole round and 30 minutes in the arcade. Ask about age rules if anyone is under 21, since it calls the place geared toward adults.
- **Walk-ins.** At Map Room Lounge, The Street Bar and Bukowski Tavern, have the first person to arrive ask for a table, then text the group the wait.

## Where should everyone meet first?

Use the station most of the group rides through, then walk together. The Street Bar is about 21 minutes on foot from Hynes.

- **B, C or D.** Use [Hynes](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl) for the west end of Boylston Street.
- **E.** This branch skips Hynes. [Prudential](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-prmnl) is about 6 minutes from Bukowski Tavern.
- **Orange Line and commuter rail.** [Back Bay station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta) is about 5 minutes from Map Room Lounge.
- **Red Line.** Change at Park Street. [Arlington](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-armnl) is two stops from there.

Friends starting at The Street Bar can wait by the George Washington statue in the [Public Garden](https://www.boston.gov/parks/public-garden), across Arlington Street. Check the MBTA's [subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) for weekend changes before anyone leaves.

## How does Sunday change the plan?

Sunday starts later on the T, and three of the seven close earlier than on Saturday. Swingers closes at 10:30 p.m., and Sabina Mezcaleria and The Street Bar close at 11 p.m.

The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) lists a first train around 6 a.m. on Sunday. It says to reach a downtown station such as Park Street by 12:35 a.m. on Sunday night for the last connections, compared with 1:30 a.m. on Saturday night.

## Common questions

### What can friends do in Back Bay when it rains?

Start with Map Room Lounge or Swingers Back Bay, since both are indoors. Swingers opens at noon on both weekend days.

### Where can friends hang out in Back Bay without paying for an activity?

The Public Garden and the Prudential Center's South Garden cost nothing to visit. Both are outdoors, so save them for a dry day.

### Which Back Bay spots stay open latest on a weekend?

Back Bay Social and A.T. O'Keeffe's stay open until 2 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday. Bukowski Tavern closes at 1 a.m. on both days.
