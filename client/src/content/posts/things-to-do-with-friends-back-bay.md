The best daytime things to do with friends in Back Bay fit on one walk, from the Mapparium at the Christian Science Plaza to the Public Garden. It takes about five hours on a Saturday, with three ticketed stops and three free ones. Your group has already agreed on Back Bay. Now someone has to turn that into a start time, a lunch and a plan that still works if it rains.

This post lays out that afternoon stop by stop, then covers what costs money, the rainy-day swap and which T stops to use. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay).

## What are the best daytime things to do with friends in Back Bay?

Walk the neighborhood from southwest to east, with a globe, a lunch, a church, a library and two parks on the way.

## What does a Saturday afternoon look like, hour by hour?

Start by noon so the ticketed stops are done before they close. Here is an example plan:

1. **11:30 a.m.** Meet at the reflecting pool.
2. **11:45 a.m.** Walk about 2 minutes to the Mapparium for your timed ticket.
3. **12:30 p.m.** Walk about 13 minutes to Eataly for lunch.
4. **2 p.m.** Walk about 13 minutes to Trinity Church.
5. **3 p.m.** Cross Copley Square, about 5 minutes, to Map Room Lounge.
6. **4 p.m.** Walk about 9 minutes to the Commonwealth Avenue Mall and follow it east.
7. **4:45 p.m.** Reach the Public Garden, then Arlington station.

The order matters. Trinity Church tours end at 4:30 p.m. and Map Room Lounge closes at 4:45 p.m., so leave the parks for last. They stay open into the evening.

## Which stops are free, and which need a ticket?

Three stops cost money, and three cost nothing.

- **Ticketed.** The Mapparium is $6 for adults, sold on the [Christian Science visitor page](https://christianscience.com/find-us/visit-the-mother-church/visit-the-christian-science-plaza/how-do-you-see-the-world-experience/how-do-you-see-the-world-visitor-information). Trinity Church is $10 for adults at the door, with seasonal days on its [tours page](https://trinitychurchboston.org/tours/). Lunch and the Map Room Lounge are the other spending.
- **Free.** The reflecting pool, the Commonwealth Avenue Mall and the Public Garden. The galleries beside the Mapparium are free too, and so is the [Central Library](https://www.bpl.org/tours/) around Map Room Lounge.

A group on a tight budget can skip Trinity Church and spend that hour in the library.

## What if it rains?

Swap the two parks for Swingers Back Bay. It's about a 5-minute walk from Map Room Lounge and opens at noon on weekends.

The Mapparium, Eataly, Trinity Church and Map Room Lounge are already indoors, so only the walks between them get wet. The library is free and open until 5 p.m. on Saturday, so a wet group can stay there longer before golf.

## Which T stops should everyone use?

Arrive at one end and leave from the other.

- **Getting there.** [Symphony](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-symcl) and [Prudential](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-prmnl) on the Green Line's E branch are both about 5 minutes from the reflecting pool. B, C and D riders use [Hynes](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl), about 7 minutes away.
- **Getting home.** [Arlington](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-armnl) has all four Green Line branches. Orange Line riders can walk to [Back Bay station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta) instead.

Check the MBTA's [subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) the day before. ## Common questions

### Does this plan work on a Sunday?

Mostly. Trinity Church has no tours on Sunday, and the Mapparium opens at 11 a.m. Swap in more time at the library, which opens at 11 a.m. on Sunday.

### How long does the Mapparium take?

The narrated show inside the globe runs about 10 minutes. Allow half an hour with the free galleries around it.

### Can we do this plan with kids?

Yes. Anyone under 18 enters the Mapparium free, and children under 14 enter Trinity Church free. Visitors under 12 need an adult in the Mapparium.
