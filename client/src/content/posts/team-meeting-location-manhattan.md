A good team meeting location in Manhattan sits a few minutes' walk from a station on the lines your team already rides. Choose the area by where people start, then pick a meeting room or a restaurant in it. Your team might be hybrid with no office of its own, or the office might be a long ride for half the people coming in.

This post covers which areas suit a team meeting, seven places across them, and when to book a room instead of a table. It is part of our guide to [New York](/blog/new-york).

## Which areas of Manhattan make a good team meeting location?

Lower Manhattan, SoHo, Broadway from Union Square to NoMad, and Midtown each have stations where many lines stop. The seven picks spread across all four, and each card gives the walk from its station.

:::places

In this post, the hours, reservations, price levels, walk times and which places are good for groups come from Google Maps as of October 2026.

## How do you choose the area when the team is spread out?

Start from where each person rides in, then pick the area most of them can reach without changing trains.

**Lower Manhattan.** Fulton St has the 2, 3, 4, 5, A, C and J, and PATH trains from Newark, Hoboken and Jersey City end at [World Trade Center](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/stations/world-trade-center-station.html). Our only pick here is a restaurant, so for a room downtown, search around Fulton St in Where2Meet. For dinner afterward, see our [group dinner spots in Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan/how-to-pick-a-restaurant-for-a-group-dinner).

![](equitable-building-entrance-120-broadway.jpg)

**SoHo.** The Canal St complex joins four stations, and the N and Q reach it across the Manhattan Bridge from Brooklyn. A few blocks north, Spring St has the 6 and Prince St has the R and W.

**Union Square to NoMad.** This stretch of Broadway runs from [Union Square](/blog/new-york/union-square) at 14th Street to about 31st Street, passing the Flatiron Building where it crosses Fifth Avenue. Hoboken and Jersey City PATH trains run up Sixth Avenue, with stops at [14 St](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/stations/14-street-station.html) and 23 St.

![](flatiron-building-fifth-avenue-sidewalk-clock.jpg)

**Midtown.** Of the four areas, Midtown has the most lines, at Times Sq-42 St, Grand Central and Herald Square. For rooms and lunches there, see [where to hold a team meeting in Midtown](/blog/new-york/midtown/how-to-choose-a-team-meeting-location). Our one Midtown pick is for people who arrive at Penn Station on LIRR, NJ Transit or Amtrak.

## Should you book a meeting room or a restaurant table?

Book a room when the agenda needs a screen, a whiteboard or a closed door. Book a table when the meeting is mostly talk.

The Farm Soho rents its rooms by the hour, as [its site](https://www.thefarmsoho.com/) says, and Union Square Loft takes bookings on [its own page](https://unionsquareloft.com/book/).

Balthazar and The Smith open at 8 a.m. on weekdays, early enough for a breakfast meeting. The Capital Grille opens at 11:30 a.m., so save it for lunch. To keep the team off the train between the meeting and the meal, book both in one area. The Farm Soho is about a 6-minute walk from Balthazar, and Blender Space is about an 8-minute walk from The Smith.

A Saturday meeting narrows the list. Blender Space is closed on weekends, and The Capital Grille opens at 4 p.m. on Saturdays. The W doesn't run on weekends either, so look up the MTA's [planned service changes](https://www.mta.info/alerts) for that weekend.

## Common questions

### How close to a station should a team meeting be?

Within a few minutes' walk. Every pick in this post is within a 3-minute walk of a subway entrance.

### Which area works if most of the team rides the L?

Union Square. The L runs there directly from Williamsburg and Bushwick in Brooklyn, and the same station has the 4, 5, 6, N, Q, R and W.

### Can a team meet at a restaurant instead of renting a room?

Yes, if the meeting needs no screen. Book ahead, and say how many people are coming.
