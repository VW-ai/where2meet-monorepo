If you need a watch party bar in Back Bay, start with the bars within a 6-minute walk of Hynes Convention Center station, on the Green Line's B, C and D branches. Five of the seven picks below are there, and two more sit by Copley and Back Bay stations. Your friends are coming from Allston, Cambridge and Jamaica Plain, the game starts at 8, and someone has to hold a table.

This post lists seven bars that show sports, then covers which ones book tables, what changes when the Red Sox play at home and how to get home after a late finish. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay). For bars near TD Garden and downtown, see [where to watch the game in Boston with friends](/blog/boston/where-to-watch-the-game-boston).

## Where is the best watch party bar in Back Bay?

Near Hynes, if most of the group rides the B, C or D branch. Sweeney's by Copley covers E-branch riders, and Clerys by Back Bay station covers the Orange Line.

## Which Back Bay bars take reservations for a big game?

Five of the seven do. Book early for a playoff game or a big rivalry, and ask for a table that faces a screen.

- **Book a table.** A.T. O'Keeffe's, Dillon's, Back Bay Social, Sweeney's and Clerys all take reservations.
- **Walk in.** Bukowski Tavern and Corner Tavern take no bookings. Send the person who lives closest 45 minutes before kickoff to hold the table.
- **Check for a band.** A.T. O'Keeffe's and Back Bay Social have live music. If the group wants to hear the commentary, ask whether a band is booked that night.

## What changes on a Red Sox home day?

The Hynes bars get busier, because Fenway Park is close. It's about a 12-minute walk from [Hynes Convention Center station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl), and [Kenmore](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-kencl) is the next stop west on the B, C and D branches.

Check the [Red Sox schedule](https://www.mlb.com/redsox/schedule) before you pick. If your game clashes with a home game at Fenway, choose Sweeney's by Copley or Clerys by [Back Bay station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta). Both sit farther east, away from the crowds heading to Kenmore.

## When do these bars open, and how late can you stay?

On weekends all seven open between 11 a.m. and noon, and six stay open until 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. That covers an afternoon kickoff and a West Coast game that ends after midnight.

None of the seven opens before 11 a.m., so a morning match from Europe needs another plan. For a late game, watch the trains as well as the clock. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Hynes is four stops from Park Street, so leave a little earlier than that.

## Common questions

### Which Back Bay bars stay open late for a Sunday night game?

A.T. O'Keeffe's, Back Bay Social, Sweeney's and Corner Tavern stay open until 2 a.m. on Sunday. Dillon's and Bukowski Tavern close at 1 a.m. Clerys closes at 8 p.m., so skip it for a night game.

### Can you watch a game outside in Back Bay?

Dillon's, Back Bay Social, Corner Tavern and Sweeney's have outdoor seating. Ask before you book whether a screen faces the patio, because the cards don't say.

### Is there a sports bar near Fenway Park in Back Bay?

A.T. O'Keeffe's is a sports bar on Boylston Street, about a 5-minute walk from Hynes Convention Center station. From there, Fenway Park is about a 15-minute walk.
