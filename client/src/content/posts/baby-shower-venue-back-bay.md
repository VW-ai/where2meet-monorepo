A baby shower venue in Back Bay with a private room lets you book by seat count, so nobody hopes a restaurant table stretches to fit. The six rooms here seat from 22 to 100, each within about a 10-minute walk of a T station. The reply list keeps growing, the parent-to-be wants to skip stairs, and someone has to drive the gifts home.

This part of our [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay) guide lists six rooms, then covers how many seats to ask for, stations with elevators, the gift car and dates to skip.

## How many seats should you ask for?

Count the yes replies and the maybes, then ask for the smallest room that holds both. Ask for the food and drink minimum too, since it can grow with the room.

- **Up to 25.** Atlantic Fish's Board Room, or Davio's Wine Room for 12 to 24.
- **25 to 40.** The Wine Rooms at Bistro du Midi and Sonsie, or one 40-seat room at Davio's.
- **40 to 65.** The Newbury Salon West, the French Library's Founders' Room or Bistro du Midi's Bistro.
- **More than 65.** Davio's two combined rooms, or a seated dinner at the French Library.

Before you sign, ask how early you can set up the gift table, whether a bakery cake is allowed and when the room must be clear.

## Which Back Bay station is step-free for the parent-to-be?

Use Arlington, Copley or Prudential, which have elevators. The MBTA lists [Hynes Convention Center as not accessible](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl).

- **Arlington.** Every Green Line branch stops here, and four of the rooms are within about an 8-minute walk.
- **Copley.** Also on every branch, and closest to Atlantic Fish.
- **Prudential.** E branch only. Use it instead of Hynes for Sonsie, about 9 minutes on foot.

Check the MBTA's [elevator alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/access) that morning, and ask the venue which door has no steps.

## Where can the gift car park and load?

Use valet or a garage, because Back Bay meters have short limits. The [city's meter page](https://www.boston.gov/node/551) lists them at $3.75 an hour, mostly Monday to Saturday from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., with some sections capped at 30 minutes. Sundays and city holidays are free.

Atlantic Fish has valet parking, Davio's and Bistro du Midi have paid garages nearby, and Sonsie has paid street parking. Have a helper carry the gifts in while the driver parks.

## Which dates should you avoid in Back Bay?

Skip Marathon Monday, and check Open Newbury before you book a Sunday at Sonsie.

- **Marathon Monday.** The [Boston Marathon](https://www.baa.org/races/boston-marathon) runs on the third Monday in April and finishes on Boylston Street near Copley Square, where streets close to cars.
- **Open Newbury.** On some Sundays the city closes Newbury Street to cars from Berkeley Street to Massachusetts Avenue, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. The [Open Newbury page](https://www.boston.gov/departments/transportation/open-newbury-street) posts the dates. Sonsie is on that stretch.

## Common questions

### How many guests can a Back Bay baby shower room hold?

[Davio's](https://davios.com/bos/private-dining) combines two rooms for up to 100, and [the French Library](https://frenchlibrary.org/rentals/) seats up to 80 for a dinner. [Bistro du Midi](https://www.bistrodumidi.com/private-dining/) lists up to 65 in its Bistro, [The Newbury](https://www.thenewburyboston.com/meetings-events) 50, [Sonsie](https://www.sonsieboston.com/private-events/) 36 and [Atlantic Fish](https://www.atlanticfish.com/group-dining/) 22.

### Can you hold a morning baby shower in a Back Bay private room?

Yes. Sonsie opens at 9:30 a.m. on weekends, and Bistro du Midi offers brunch packages. Davio's opens at 3 p.m. on weekends, so save it for a weekday or an afternoon.

### Can you hold a weekday baby shower in Back Bay?

Yes. Davio's, Atlantic Fish and Bistro du Midi all open at 11:30 a.m. on weekdays, which suits a lunch shower for coworkers.
