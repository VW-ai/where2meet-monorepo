The right baby shower venue in Back Bay depends on your reply count. A reserved table suits a small group, and a room booked by date suits a larger one. You don't know yet who's coming, the parent-to-be wants to avoid stairs, and someone has to get a carload of gifts home.

This post, part of our [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay) guide, lists seven places, then covers stations with elevators, parking for the gift car and the dates that close streets. For picks across the city, see [where to have a baby shower in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-have-baby-shower-boston).

## Which Back Bay station is easiest for the parent-to-be?

Use Arlington or Copley when stairs are a problem. Both have elevators and serve all four Green Line branches, while [Hynes has no elevators](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl).

- **Arlington and Copley.** Five of the seven picks are within about a 5-minute walk of one or the other.
- **Prudential.** Rochambeau and Sonsie sit closest to Hynes, so the step-free choice is Prudential, about 7 and 9 minutes on foot. Only the E branch stops there.
- **Back Bay.** Relatives on the Orange Line or the commuter rail can get off here. The Courtyard Tea Room is about a 5-minute walk.

The [Arlington station page](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-armnl) shows whether each elevator is working, so check it that morning. Ask the venue about steps too, since the College Club is a row house and The Tower has four floors.

## Where can guests park and load the gifts?

Send the gift driver to a garage, because some Back Bay meters have short limits. The [city's meter page](https://www.boston.gov/node/551) lists Back Bay meters at $3.75 an hour, Monday to Saturday from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Some sections stop at 30 minutes, and the south side of Boylston Street between Arlington and Clarendon stops at 15. Sundays and city holidays are free, but other parking rules still apply.

The [Prudential Center garage](https://www.prudentialcenter.com/parking) is open 24 hours. With a $25 purchase at Prudential Center and entry after 9:30 a.m., up to four hours cost $20. Ask your venue about its own validation or valet.

## Which dates should you avoid in Back Bay?

Skip Marathon Monday, and check Open Newbury before you book a Sunday on Newbury Street.

- **Marathon Monday.** The B.A.A. has [set the 2027 race](https://www.baa.org/news/registration-updates-and-information-announced-for-2027-boston-marathon-presented-by-bank-of-america/) for Monday, April 19. In 2026 the MBTA [closed Copley all day](https://www.mbta.com/news/2026-04-16/mbta-announces-service-information-the-2026-boston-marathon) and pointed riders to Arlington, Prudential, Hynes and Back Bay. No 2027 notice is out yet.
- **Open Newbury.** In 2026 the city [closed Newbury Street to cars](https://www.boston.gov/news/2026-open-newbury-schedule-announced) from Berkeley Street to Massachusetts Avenue on ten Sundays, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Sonsie sits on that stretch, so no car can drop a guest at its door during those hours. The city hasn't posted 2027 dates.

## Common questions

### How many guests can these Back Bay venues seat?

[The Tower](https://longwoodvenues.com/venues/boston-historic-event-venue/) seats up to 180 with a program, and [the College Club](https://thecollegeclubofboston.com/events/) seats 60. [Rochambeau](https://rochambeauboston.com/private-dining) seats 75 in its full Bordeaux Room, and [Sonsie](https://www.sonsieboston.com/private-events/) seats 36 in its Wine Room. [The Newbury](https://www.thenewburyboston.com/meetings-events) and [the Copley Plaza](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/boston/fairmont-copley-plaza/weddings.html) each list rooms for 30.

### Can you have afternoon tea at a baby shower in Back Bay?

Yes. Reserve at the Courtyard Tea Room, or ask [its operator](https://www.thecateredaffair.com/library-tea/) about large groups and private events. The Newbury also [serves afternoon tea](https://www.thenewburyboston.com/dine) in The Street Bar, which takes reservations through Resy.

### Can you hold a baby shower on a Sunday in Back Bay?

Yes, and Sunday meters are free. Sonsie opens at 9:30 a.m., and the Courtyard Tea Room runs from 11 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. The College Club's listed hours end at noon, so ask before you plan around it.
