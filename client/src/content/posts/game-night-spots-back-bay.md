The best game night spots in Back Bay put the game in the room for you, from crazy golf on Boylston Street to bowling lanes by the Prudential Center. Swingers Back Bay is a 4-minute walk from Copley, and Trident Booksellers & Cafe runs trivia every Friday near Hynes. Your group wants a night out with something to play, but nobody agrees on what, and nobody wants to host.

This post lists six places, which ones turn 21+ at night, how to book for a big group, and the T stop for each. It's part of our [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay) guide.

## Which game night spots in Back Bay fit your group?

Choose by the game first. Pick golf or trivia when half the group has never played, and bowling or billiards when people want to keep score.

Back Bay has no board game bar. Three of these picks sit on the Fenway side of Massachusetts Avenue, a short walk past the neighborhood's western edge.

## Can players under 21 come along?

At some picks, and only early at others. Two of the bars turn 21+ in the evening, so check ages before you choose.

- **Lucky Strike Fenway** is 21+ after 7 p.m., [its page says](https://www.luckystrikeent.com/location/lucky-strike-boston).
- **Cheeky Monkey** is 21+ after 8 p.m., according to [its page](https://www.luckystrikeent.com/location/cheeky-monkey-brewing-co).
- **Kings** and **Swingers** don't post an age rule on the pages we could read, so ask when you book.

For a group with teenagers, plan an afternoon at the bowling lanes or Friday trivia at Trident.

## How do you book a game night for a big group?

Split the group to fit each game. Bowling and trivia both cap how many people play together.

1. **Bowling.** Lucky Strike holds up to 8 people per lane, so a group of 12 needs two lanes. Lanes open for booking 8 days ahead, and events 6 months ahead.
2. **Trivia.** Trident caps teams at 6, so 10 friends play as two teams at neighboring tables if the room has space.
3. **Billiards and bowling with dinner.** Kings and Cheeky Monkey both take reservations, and both have event planners for a private party.

## Which T stop should each player use?

Copley is closest to Swingers, Hynes to Kings and Trident, and Kenmore or Lansdowne station to the Lansdowne Street picks.

- **Green Line B, C and D riders** stop at Copley, Hynes and Kenmore.
- **E branch riders** stop at Copley and Prudential, about a 5-minute walk from Kings, but skip Hynes and Kenmore.
- **Commuter rail riders** on the Framingham/Worcester line stop at Lansdowne, about 3 minutes on foot from Game On!.

![](kenmore-station-green-line-train-platform.jpg)

Four picks stay open to 1 a.m. or later on weekends, past the last connections. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

## Common questions

### Is there a board game bar near me in Back Bay?

Not on this list. The nearest board game cafes are Tavern of Tales in Mission Hill and Knight Moves in Brookline, both in our [Boston board game bar guide](/blog/boston/board-game-bar-boston).

### Which game night spots are open on a Monday?

Kings, Lucky Strike, Game On! and Trident are. Swingers is closed on Monday, and Cheeky Monkey on Monday and Tuesday.

### Where can a game night keep going after midnight?

Game On! stays open to 2 a.m. Thursday to Saturday. Lucky Strike and Cheeky Monkey close at 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, and Kings at 1 a.m.
