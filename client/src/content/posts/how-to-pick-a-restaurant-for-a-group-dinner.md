Group dinner spots in Lower Manhattan sit close to the trains most people ride home. Book near Fulton St, Wall St or Stone Street, and set the time by the person with the longest trip. Maybe the group works in the Financial District, but half of it lives in Brooklyn and two people take PATH home to Jersey City.

This post lists eight places, then covers booking and the ride home. It is part of our guide to [Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan).

## Which restaurants suit a group dinner in Lower Manhattan?

Seven of the eight take reservations, per Google, and the eight span three price levels. Each walk is from the station named.

## How do you book a table for a big group?

Book early, and ask about the restaurant's rules for groups before you share it. For a big group, call or ask about a private room. Ask whether it needs a set menu or a card deposit and how it splits checks, then share the price per person with the group.

## How will everyone get home?

Pick a place near the lines people ride home, and agree on a rough end time.

- **New Jersey.** PATH trains to Newark leave from World Trade Center at all hours. Trains to Hoboken run from about 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. on weekdays and 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on weekends, as the [PATH schedules](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/schedules-maps.html) say.
- **Staten Island.** The [Staten Island Ferry](https://siferry.com/) is free and runs 24 hours a day from the Whitehall terminal.
- **Late at night.** The 3, 5, C, W and Z don't run here. The 2, 4, A, E, J and 1 run all night.

## Common questions

### Which station is best for a group dinner in the Financial District?

Fulton St suits most groups, with the 2, 3, 4, 5, A, C and J. Wall St, on the 2 and 3, is closer to Stone Street.

### Is Stone Street closed to cars?

Two of its blocks are, near Hanover Square. Restaurants set tables out on them, as the cover photo shows.

### Can friends from Staten Island stay late?

Yes. The Staten Island Ferry runs 24 hours a day from the Whitehall terminal, and it is free.
