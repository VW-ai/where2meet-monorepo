If you're deciding where to watch the game in Boston with a group, start at the bars around Haymarket station, where the Orange Line and the Green Line's D and E branches stop. Two of the picks below are a 3- or 4-minute walk from it. Your friends live in Somerville, Quincy and Brookline, and nobody wants to watch kickoff alone on a phone.

This post lists seven bars set up for watching sports, from Haymarket to the Seaport. It covers which stops to use, how to hold a table for a big group and how to get home after a late game. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which watch party bar in Boston fits your group?

Pick by the T line most of the group rides, then by how loud you want the room. The Haymarket bars suit Orange and Green line riders.

The Black Rose has live music every night, and Durty Nelly's has it on weekends, so the stage can drown out the game. If the group wants to hear the commentary, pick a bar without a band.

## Where to watch the game on a Celtics or Bruins home night?

Away from TD Garden, unless your group is ready for a crowd. The Garden sits above North Station, about a 10-minute walk from the Haymarket picks, and the Celtics and Bruins play their home games there.

On those nights, ticket holders fill the same few blocks. Check the [Celtics schedule](https://www.nba.com/celtics/schedule) and the [Bruins schedule](https://www.nhl.com/bruins/schedule) before you choose. If your game isn't the one at the Garden, Corner Pub by South Station or 21st Amendment on Beacon Hill keeps you away from the arena traffic.

## How do you hold a table for a big watch party?

Book where you can, and send one person early where you can't. When you book, ask for a table that faces a screen.

The Point, The Black Rose and F1 Arcade all take reservations. F1 Arcade's [Boston page](https://f1arcade.com/us/boston) also offers semi-private rooms and private hire for larger groups. Corner Pub has a [party request form](https://cornerpubboston.net/party/). Durty Nelly's, J.J. Donovan's and 21st Amendment take no bookings, so the first person there holds the table.

## How do you get home after a late game?

Pick a bar near a transfer station and watch the clock. The subway doesn't run all night, and a game that starts at 10 p.m. Eastern can end after the last connection.

The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. J.J. Donovan's is close to State, and 21st Amendment is close to Park Street, where the Red Line meets every Green Line branch.

For a night without a screen, see [where to hang out with friends in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-hang-out-with-friends-boston). If the group would rather play than watch, see our [board game bar and game night ideas in Boston](/blog/boston/board-game-bar-boston).

## Common questions

### Where can you watch Formula 1 races in Boston?

F1 Arcade in the Seaport shows every Grand Prix live on big screens, its Boston page says. It also lists watch parties for some races.

### Can you bring kids to a watch party bar in Boston?

F1 Arcade welcomes all ages, though guests need to be 7 to race, according to its website. For the other bars, call ahead and ask about age rules before you bring anyone under 21.

### Which of these bars work for a Sunday night game?

Durty Nelly's, The Black Rose, 21st Amendment and J.J. Donovan's stay open past midnight on Sunday. Corner Pub closes at 10 p.m. on Sunday, so pick another bar for a late kickoff.
