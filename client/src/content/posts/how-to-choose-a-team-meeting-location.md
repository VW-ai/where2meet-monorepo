Where to hold a team meeting in Midtown depends on the station most of your team rides into. Book a room or a table a few minutes' walk from Times Sq-42 St, Grand Central, Herald Square or Bryant Park, since those stations serve 18 subway lines plus the 42 St Shuttle on weekdays between them. Maybe half the team commutes from Brooklyn and two people take Metro-North in from Westchester. A spot by the right station gives most of them a ride with no transfer.

This post lists eight places to meet and what changes after work. It is part of our guide to [Midtown](/blog/new-york/midtown).

## Where to hold a team meeting in Midtown?

Pick by what the meeting needs, from a room with a door to a lunch the whole team can share. Each walk is from the station named.

## What changes after work and on weekends?

Fewer trains run here at night and on weekends, and some picks close. On weekends, the B, M and W don't run through Midtown, and Convene, Vanderbilt Market and the Oyster Bar are closed. Late at night, the B, C, M, R, W and 5 don't run here either, and the 42 St Shuttle stops from about midnight to 6 a.m., as its [MTA timetable](https://www.mta.info/schedules/subway/42-st-shuttle) says.

## How to do it in Where2Meet

Where2Meet lets a team plan where to meet from one shared link. Nobody needs an account.

1. Open [Where2Meet](/), choose the **Team meeting** chip, pick the date and time, and add your name.
2. Share the link. Each person adds where they're coming from, or an approximate area, or you add it for them.
3. Search for rooms or restaurants around the group, or look one up by name.
4. Select a place to see each person's route and travel time by car, transit, walking or bike.
5. Everyone votes, and the organizer picks the final spot. Changes show up without a refresh.

## Common questions

### Which Midtown station gives the most people a direct ride?

Times Sq-42 St has the 1, 2, 3, 7, N, Q, R and W, plus the A, C and E at its Eighth Avenue end. 34 St-Herald Sq has the B, D, F, M, N, Q, R and W.

### Where should teammates from New Jersey get off?

PATH trains from Hoboken and Jersey City stop at 33 St, next to Herald Square. A lunch near Herald Square, such as miss KOREA BBQ, keeps their walk short.

### Where do teammates on Metro-North arrive?

Metro-North trains end at Grand Central Terminal, where the 4, 5, 6 and 7 also stop. The Campbell, the Oyster Bar and Vanderbilt Market are each a few minutes' walk away.
