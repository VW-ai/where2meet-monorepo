A board game bar is the easiest place in Boston to meet for a game night, because the games, tables and drinks are already there. Tavern of Tales, a board game cafe and bar in Mission Hill, is a 3-minute walk from Roxbury Crossing on the Orange Line. Your friends live in Brookline, Somerville and Dorchester. Somebody owns Catan, but nobody has a table that seats six.

This post lists two board game cafes and four bars built around other games. Then it covers age rules, booking a big group and which T stops work late at night. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which board game bar in Boston fits your group?

Pick a board game cafe when the group wants to sit and play one game for hours. Pick a game bar when people want to stand, move around and switch games.

The two board game cafes are on opposite sides of the city. Tavern of Tales suits riders on the Orange Line and the Green Line's E branch, which stops at Brigham Circle about a 9-minute walk away. Knight Moves suits people in Brookline, Allston and Brighton.

## Can everyone in the group get in after dark?

Not at every pick. Three of the bars turn 21+ in the evening, so check the ages in your group before you choose.

- **Flight Club** is 21+ after 6 p.m. Sunday to Friday and all day Saturday, as its [Boston page](https://www.flightclubdartsusa.com/boston) says.
- **VERSUS** is 21+ after 7 p.m., according to [its website](https://www.versusboston.com/).
- **Kings** is 21+ after 9 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, its [Seaport page](https://playatkings.com/location/boston-seaport) says.
- **Tavern of Tales** has no age limit, but its [website](https://www.tavernoftales.com/) asks that anyone under 18 come with an adult.

For a group with teenagers, book Tavern of Tales or play at Kings before 9 p.m.

## How do you book a game night for a big group?

Book ahead wherever you can, and send one person early where you can't. A table for 10 is harder to find on a Friday than a table for four.

Tavern of Tales rents private game rooms online and asks parties of seven or more to email ahead. SPIN's [Boston page](https://wearespin.com/location/boston/) takes table bookings for up to 10 people. Flight Club books groups of 2 to 12 online and plans events for 13 or more. Knight Moves takes no bookings, so the first person there should claim the table.

## Which T stops make game night easy to reach?

Match the place to the line most of the group rides. Flight Club and Kings share one building by the Courthouse stop, a short Silver Line ride from South Station.

VERSUS is the most central pick. Downtown Crossing has the Red and Orange lines, and Park Street adds every Green Line branch. Knight Moves stays open until 2 a.m. on weekends, later than the subway runs. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday to make the last connections.

## Common questions

### Is there a board game bar near me in Boston?

Tavern of Tales in Mission Hill and Knight Moves in Brookline's Coolidge Corner are the two board game cafes on this list. Downtown, VERSUS keeps board games next to its arcade machines.

### Where can you play board games late at night in Boston?

Knight Moves stays open until 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Every other night it closes at 11 p.m.

### Which game night spots are open on Monday?

Five of the six are. SPIN is closed on Sunday and Monday, so pick another place for the start of the week.
