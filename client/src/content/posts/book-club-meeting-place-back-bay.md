The best book club meeting place in Back Bay is a table you can reserve, in a room that stays open past your last question. Trident Booksellers & Cafe on Newbury Street fits most evening clubs, and the two tea rooms inside the Boston Public Library suit daytime ones. You have a date and a title, members are coming from different T lines, and you need somewhere eight people can talk for two hours.

This post, part of our [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay) guide, lists seven places, then matches them to the times clubs meet and the T stops members use. For picks in Beacon Hill, the Seaport and Charlestown, see [where to host a book club in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-host-book-club-boston).

## Which book club meeting place in Back Bay fits your club?

Start with your head count and whether you want a meal. Once your club reaches six, choose a place that takes reservations, so nobody spends the first half hour hunting for chairs.

## Which picks work for the day your club meets?

Match your usual start time to the closing time before you send the date. Three picks suit a 7 p.m. start from Monday to Thursday, and the library's tea rooms suit earlier meetings.

- **Weeknights at 7 p.m.** Trident, Piattini and Back Bay Social all stay open at least two and a half hours after that. Trident runs [its own book clubs](https://tridentbookscafe.com/events) from 7 to 9 p.m. on some weeknights, so mention yours when you book.
- **Wednesday or Thursday after work.** Both tea rooms stay open to 7 p.m., so a 5 p.m. start gets you two hours.
- **Weekend afternoons.** Both tea rooms close at 4:45 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday, so start by 2:30 p.m.
- **Friday night.** Thinking Cup stays open to 9 p.m., which gives a club of three or four a coffee shop option after work.

## Which T stop should members aim for?

Send each member to the stop that matches the pick, because the Green Line branches split west of Copley. Copley has all four branches and is the closest stop to the library, Thinking Cup and Piattini.

- **B, C and D riders.** Stay on to Hynes for Trident and Back Bay Social.
- **E riders.** Get off at Copley, since the E doesn't stop at Hynes.
- **Orange Line and commuter rail riders.** Back Bay station is about a 5-minute walk from the library, but about 17 minutes from Trident. Those members may prefer a pick on Copley Square.
- **Members who need an elevator.** [Hynes has no elevators](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl). Prudential, on the E branch, has step-free access and is about a 10-minute walk from Trident.

A club that stays late at Back Bay Social should check the MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) for the last connections.

## Common questions

### Can a book club book a private room in Back Bay?

Yes. Trident's [The Stacks](https://tridentbookscafe.com/private-events) is 1,350 square feet of event space among the second-floor bookshelves, booked through an inquiry form. Its page doesn't list a minimum spend, so ask before you commit.

### Can a book club reserve a free room at the Boston Public Library?

Usually not. The library's [community rooms](https://www.bpl.org/reservable-community-spaces/) are for nonprofits holding meetings that are free and open to the public. A private club can meet at either tea room in the building instead.

### Where can a book club meet on a Monday in Back Bay?

Skip the library, because both tea rooms are closed on Monday. Trident, Thinking Cup, Piattini and Back Bay Social are all open.
