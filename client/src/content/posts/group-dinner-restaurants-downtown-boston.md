The best group dinner restaurants in Downtown Boston take reservations, seat groups and sit a few minutes from a T stop. Downtown runs from South Station to North Station, so pick a table near the station that most of your guests already ride to. You're booking for coworkers or old friends who come in on different subway lines, and a few of them take the commuter rail home.

This post suggests seven restaurants, then covers which station to plan around, which nights work and how to book for a group. It's part of our guide to [Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown).

## Which group dinner restaurants in Downtown Boston fit your group?

Start with the station most of your guests ride to, then check the night. All seven are good for groups, and six take reservations.

## Which station should guests ride to?

Count who rides which line, then choose the restaurant closest to the station most of them reach without a transfer. Downtown's stations sit a few blocks apart, and each has a different mix of lines.

- **Red and Orange.** [Downtown Crossing](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-dwnxg) is about 2 minutes from Estella and about 4 from Elephant & Castle and Stillwater.
- **Green.** [Park Street](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-pktrm) has all four branches, and Estella is about 3 minutes from it.
- **Orange and Blue.** [State](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-state) is about 2 minutes from Elephant & Castle.
- **Blue only.** [Aquarium](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-aqucl) serves the three waterfront picks, Waterline, Central Wharf Co. and Boston Sail Loft.
- **Commuter rail from the south and west.** [South Station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-sstat) is about 5 minutes from Stillwater. The other picks are 9 minutes or more away.
- **Commuter rail from the north.** [North Station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-north) is about 3 minutes from Causeway Boston, which is the only pick near it.

## Which nights work, and how late can you stay?

Every pick but Central Wharf Co. serves dinner seven nights a week. Closing times range from 9:30 p.m. to 2 a.m., so match the restaurant to the length of the night.

- **An early dinner.** Stillwater closes at 9:30 p.m. Sunday to Wednesday. Book a 6:30 p.m. table there.
- **A long meal.** Waterline stays open until 11 p.m. and Elephant & Castle until midnight, every night.
- **A night that runs past midnight.** Boston Sail Loft and Causeway Boston are open until 2 a.m. every day. Estella and Central Wharf Co. stay open that late on Friday and Saturday.

The subway closes before the late restaurants do. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station such as Park Street or Downtown Crossing by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Commuter rail riders should look up their last train on the [commuter rail schedules](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/commuter-rail) before you set the start time.

## How do you book a table for the whole group?

Reserve a few days ahead and give the restaurant an exact headcount. Ask whether everyone sits at one table or across two, and whether a large party has a minimum spend or a set menu.

As an example, a group of 12 that wants one long table on a Friday should call rather than book online, so the restaurant can say whether it seats everyone together. Boston Sail Loft is the one pick to call first, since we couldn't confirm that it takes reservations.

## Common questions

### Which Downtown Boston restaurants stay open late for a group?

Boston Sail Loft and Causeway Boston are open until 2 a.m. every day. Estella and Central Wharf Co. stay open until 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

### Where can a group eat downtown on a Monday night?

Six of the seven picks are open on Monday. Central Wharf Co. is the exception, since it's closed Sunday through Tuesday.

### Which restaurant is closest to South Station?

Stillwater, about a 5-minute walk away on Kingston Street. Downtown Crossing on the Red and Orange lines is about 4 minutes from it.
