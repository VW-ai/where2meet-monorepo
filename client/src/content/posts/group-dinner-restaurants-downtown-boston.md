The best group dinner restaurants for an after-work team in Downtown Boston sit a few minutes from the station that takes most of you home. Book near South Station or North Station when several people ride the commuter rail, and near Downtown Crossing or State when most ride the subway. You're taking coworkers to dinner straight from the office, and a few of them can't miss the last train to Worcester, Lowell or Kingston.

This post suggests seven restaurants by station, then covers which terminal to plan around, how late the table can stay, and how to book from the office. It's part of our guide to [Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown).

## Which group dinner restaurants are closest to your trains?

Two sit by South Station, two by North Station and three by the subway stations in between. All seven take reservations and are good for groups.

## Should you book near South Station or North Station?

Book near the terminal that the most commuter rail riders use. The two stations are more than 20 minutes apart on foot, so no table is a short walk from both.

- **South Station.** Every line to the south and west leaves from here, including Framingham/Worcester, Needham, Franklin/Foxboro, Providence/Stoughton, Fall River/New Bedford, Kingston and Greenbush. Book Trade or Stillwater.
- **North Station.** The Fitchburg, Haverhill, Lowell and Newburyport/Rockport lines leave from here. Book Causeway Boston or Filippo.
- **Both.** Book The Merchant or Elephant & Castle, about 9 minutes on foot from South Station. North-side riders take the Orange Line from State or Downtown Crossing to North Station.

The MBTA's [commuter rail map](https://www.mbta.com/cr-map) shows each line's Boston terminal.

## How late can you stay before the last train?

Work back from the earliest last train in the group. Commuter rail lines end service at different times, and weekend schedules differ from weekday ones.

1. Ask each rider for the last train they can take, or look it up on the [commuter rail schedules](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/commuter-rail).
2. Take the earliest one and subtract the walk on that restaurant's card.
3. Leave 10 minutes to find the track, then end dinner there.

As an example, a Haverhill Line rider whose last train leaves North Station at 10:30 p.m. can stay at Causeway Boston until about 10:15 p.m. At The Merchant, the same rider would leave by about 10 p.m. to make the Orange Line connection.

Subway riders have more room. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown station such as Downtown Crossing, State, Park Street or North Station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, or 1:30 a.m. on Friday or Saturday, for the last connections.

## How do you book a table straight from the office?

Book a 5:30 or 6 p.m. table a few days ahead, so the group walks over together and the riders with early trains get a full meal.

- **Pick the night.** Trade is closed Sunday and Monday, and The Merchant on Sunday. The other five serve dinner every night.
- **Give a firm headcount.** For 10 or more, call instead of booking online, and ask whether everyone fits at one table.
- **Settle the check early.** Ask for one bill and split it before the first rider leaves.

## Common questions

### Which downtown restaurant is closest to South Station?

Trade, about a 4-minute walk on Atlantic Avenue, though it's closed Sunday and Monday. Stillwater on Kingston Street is about a 5-minute walk and open every day.

### Where can a group eat near North Station after work?

Causeway Boston is about a 3-minute walk and open until 2 a.m. every day. Filippo Ristorante & Bar is about 4 minutes away and closes at 10 p.m. most weeknights.

### Can a team eat downtown on a Monday night?

Yes. Six of the seven picks are open on Monday. Trade is the exception.
