The best places for a group dinner in the Mission District sit close to the BART station most of your group rides to. Seven places for a group dinner below fall between the 16th St Mission and 24th St Mission stations, and six of them take a booking. You're organizing dinner for eight or ten people. A few come from the East Bay, a few from across the city, and nobody has agreed on a budget yet.

This guide lists the seven, then covers which end of the neighborhood to pick, what each one costs, and which nights to avoid. It's part of our guide to the [Mission District](/blog/san-francisco/mission-district), in [San Francisco](/blog/san-francisco).

## Which places for a group dinner in the Mission take a booking?

Six of the seven do. El Farolito is the exception, a taqueria where the group walks in and stays as late as it likes.

## Should the group aim for the 16th Street end or the 24th Street end?

Pick the end nearest the station most of the group arrives at. The two BART stations are about eight blocks apart, and the walk between them takes about 20 minutes.

- **The 16th Street end.** Puerto Alegre and Tacolicious are closest to 16th St Mission. The 22 Fillmore bus also stops at 16th and Mission.
- **The 24th Street end.** El Farolito, Beretta, Foreign Cinema and Lolinda are each 7 minutes or less on foot from 24th St Mission.
- **The Dolores Park side.** Delfina suits guests coming from Noe Valley or the Castro on the J Church, and it's about a 12-minute walk from 16th St Mission.

Both stations serve the same four BART lines, as the [16th St Mission](https://www.bart.gov/stations/16th) and [24th St Mission](https://www.bart.gov/stations/24th) station pages show, so an East Bay rider can reach either end without a transfer. The [14 Mission](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/14-mission) bus links the two stations along Mission Street 24 hours a day.

## How much does a group dinner in the Mission cost?

The same few blocks run from inexpensive to expensive, so agree on a budget before the group votes on a place.

- **Inexpensive:** El Farolito.
- **Moderate:** Puerto Alegre, Tacolicious and Beretta.
- **Expensive:** Delfina, Foreign Cinema and Lolinda.

When the group's budgets differ, start the vote with the three moderate picks and add one expensive place only if most people want it. Ask about separate checks when you book, not when the bill comes.

![](mission-street-new-mission-theater-sign-night.jpg)

## Which nights and hours should the group plan around?

Check the day and the closing time before you send the invite. Puerto Alegre is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays and closes at 8 p.m. on Wednesdays and Thursdays. On Sundays, Delfina and Foreign Cinema stop serving dinner at 9 p.m.

For a dinner that runs late, Beretta serves until 11:30 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays, and Lolinda and Tacolicious until 11 p.m. El Farolito works as the backup when a booking falls through, since it stays open long after the others close. BART doesn't run overnight, so check the last train on its [schedules page](https://www.bart.gov/schedules) and its [service advisories](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/advisories) the day before.

## Common questions

### Can a big group walk in for dinner in the Mission?

Yes, at El Farolito, which takes no reservations and is good for groups. For a sit-down dinner on a Friday or Saturday, book one of the other six ahead.

### Which Mission restaurant is closest to BART?

El Farolito is about a 1-minute walk from 24th St Mission. Among the places that take a booking, Puerto Alegre is about 4 minutes from 16th St Mission and Beretta about 6 minutes from 24th St Mission.

### Where can a group eat in the Mission on a Monday?

Tacolicious, Beretta, Lolinda, Delfina, Foreign Cinema and El Farolito all open on Mondays. Only Puerto Alegre is closed.
