The most flexible team meeting space in San Francisco's Financial District is a room you rent by the hour near the Embarcadero or Montgomery St station. For a meal before or after, several restaurants nearby have private rooms. Your team doesn't commute one way. Some ride BART under the bay from Oakland, one takes the ferry from Larkspur, and someone comes up from Caltrain.

This post lists seven places, then covers which arrival point to plan around, how ferry and train timetables set the hours, and what changes on a weekend. It's part of our guide to the [Financial District](/blog/san-francisco/financial-district), in [San Francisco](/blog/san-francisco).

## Where can you find team meeting space in the Financial District?

Rent a coworking room for the working part, and book a restaurant room if the meeting includes a meal. The three coworking spaces open on weekdays only.

## Which arrival point should the team plan around?

Count how each person gets downtown, then pick the side of the district closest to most of them. There are four ways in, and each lands in a different spot.

- **BART riders** from the East Bay come through the Transbay Tube and stop at Embarcadero first, then Montgomery St. Both stations also have the J, K, L, M and N Muni Metro lines.
- **Ferry riders** land at the Ferry Building. [Golden Gate Ferry](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/schedules-maps/) comes from Marin, and [San Francisco Bay Ferry](https://sfbayferry.com/) comes from the East Bay and Vallejo.
- **Transbay bus riders** on AC Transit arrive at the Salesforce Transit Center, on the south side of Market Street.
- **Caltrain riders** arrive at 4th and King, outside the district. The N Judah runs from there along The Embarcadero to the Embarcadero station, as its [route page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/n-judah) shows.

When most of the team comes by water, keep the meeting near the Ferry Building and Embarcadero Center. When most ride to Montgomery St, CANOPY and Per Diem are closer. Check [BART's advisories](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/advisories) and [Golden Gate Ferry's alerts](https://www.goldengate.org/service-alerts/) the day before.

## How do ferry and train timetables set the meeting hours?

Ferry and Caltrain riders run on fixed timetables, so their boats and trains decide when the meeting can start and end. BART and Muni riders have more choice.

Ask each ferry rider for the boat they plan to catch home, and set the end time a little before it. Do the same for Caltrain riders with [Caltrain's schedules](https://www.caltrain.com/schedules). The coworking rooms keep office hours, so plan the working session for midday and move to dinner after. BART runs until about midnight, as the [Financial District page](/blog/san-francisco/financial-district) explains, along with the Owl buses that run after it.

## What if the meeting falls on a weekend?

Hold it at a restaurant. Carr, WeWork and CANOPY close on Saturday and Sunday, and Per Diem opens on weekends only for private events.

Terrene and Wayfare Tavern open every day, and Barcha serves Saturday dinner only. BART opens later on weekends, at 6 a.m. on Saturdays and 8 a.m. on Sundays, so an early Sunday start won't work for BART riders.

## Common questions

### Can you rent a meeting room by the hour in the Financial District?

Yes. Carr Workplaces rents rooms to non-members by the hour or the day, and WeWork rents rooms by the hour through its app. Both are closed on weekends.

### Which BART station is closest to the Ferry Building?

Embarcadero, at the foot of Market Street. The walk from the station to the Ferry Building takes about 4 minutes.

### Where can a team of 20 have a private lunch in the Financial District?

Book a private room through a restaurant's events form. Per Diem and Wayfare Tavern both have rooms for a group that size, and Wayfare is also open on weekends.
