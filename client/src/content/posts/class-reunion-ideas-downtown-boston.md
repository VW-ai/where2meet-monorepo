The best class reunion ideas in Downtown Boston split the weekend across a few rooms near State station. A big room on State Street holds the main night, and a tavern near Faneuil Hall holds the late hours. You're pulling together classmates who now live in different cities, ride different T lines and want different things from the night. Some want a program and name tags. Others just want a table and a late bar.

This post suggests seven places, then covers a sample weekend, how out-of-town classmates get downtown and where to take the class photo. It's part of our guide to [Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown).

## Which class reunion ideas work in Downtown Boston?

Pick one booked room for the main event and two walk-in or reservable places for the hours around it. Everything below sits between South Station and Haymarket.

## How do you spread a reunion over a weekend?

Give each night a different job, so classmates can pick the parts they want. Here is an example for a class of about 100.

1. **Friday, 6 p.m.** Welcome drinks at the Sam Adams taproom for whoever is in town. No head count needed.
2. **Saturday, 6:30 p.m.** The main dinner and program in the State Room upstairs.
3. **Saturday, 11 p.m.** Walk about 5 minutes to the Green Dragon Tavern for the late crowd.
4. **Sunday, 9 a.m.** Breakfast at Parker's Restaurant, about 6 minutes on foot from 60 State Street.

A smaller class can swap the State Room for a reserved table at Granary Tavern or Nebo. For more booked tables nearby, see our [group dinner restaurants in Downtown Boston](/blog/boston/downtown/group-dinner-restaurants-downtown-boston).

## How do out-of-town classmates get downtown?

Point everyone at State station. It has the Orange and Blue lines, and the State Room's building is about 2 minutes from it.

- **From Logan Airport,** take the shuttle bus from the terminals to Airport station, then the Blue Line to [State](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-state).
- **From the south and west,** commuter rail ends at [South Station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-sstat). Nebo is about 5 minutes away on foot.
- **From the north,** trains end at North Station. Ride the Orange Line two stops to State.

The subway closes overnight. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a transfer station by 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday for the last connections. Anyone staying at the Green Dragon until close needs a ride share or a hotel nearby.

## Where can the whole class take a photo?

Use the Latin School mosaic on School Street, a few steps from the Omni Parker House. The [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/first-public-school-site.htm) lists it as a Freedom Trail site. It marks where settlers built the first schoolhouse of the Latin School, the oldest public school in America, in 1635.

Gather there on Sunday morning on the way to or from breakfast at Parker's. Name one classmate as the photographer, and ask the group to line up behind the mosaic so the words stay in the frame.

## Common questions

### How far ahead should a class book a reunion room downtown?

Ask event venues like the State Room for dates before you announce the reunion. Booked rooms set the date, and restaurants with reservations can follow once you know the head count.

### What are low-cost class reunion ideas in Downtown Boston?

A walk-in round at the Sam Adams taproom costs only what each person orders. A Freedom Trail photo stop on School Street is free.

### Where can a class keep going after 11 p.m.?

The Green Dragon Tavern is open until 2 a.m. every night, and Warehouse Kitchen + Sports Bar until 1 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Check the last T connections before the night ends.
