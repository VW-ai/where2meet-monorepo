The most useful date night ideas in Chicago start with the hour, not the restaurant. The Loop suits an early start, the West Loop suits dinner, and River North and Wicker Park suit a night that runs late, because they sit on the two 'L' lines that never stop. Maybe you live in Logan Square, your date lives in Lincoln Square, and neither of you wants a long wait on a cold platform at midnight.

This guide picks seven places in four neighborhoods, in the order of the evening, then covers where your lines meet, getting home late and booking. It is part of our guide to [Chicago](/blog/chicago).

## Which date night ideas in Chicago fit the time you have?

Match the neighborhood to the hour. A 5 p.m. start fits the Loop, an 8 p.m. dinner fits the West Loop, and a night that goes past midnight fits River North or Wicker Park.

[The Loop](/blog/chicago/the-loop) works for a date that starts right after work. The museum and the rooftop on Michigan Avenue are about a 4-minute walk apart, so you can move from one to the other without another train.

[The West Loop](/blog/chicago/west-loop) is a dinner neighborhood. Its restaurants line Randolph Street and Fulton Market a few blocks from Morgan, so a second stop is a short walk, not a second ride.

## Where should you meet if you ride different 'L' lines?

Choose the neighborhood where both of your lines already stop, so neither of you changes trains. The Loop is the easy answer, because most lines run through it.

Clark/Lake has six lines: the Blue, Brown, Green, Orange, Pink and Purple. For example, if one of you rides the Blue Line from Logan Square and the other the Brown Line from Lincoln Square, both reach Clark/Lake without a transfer. The Loop and River North are then a short walk or one stop away.

When you both live on the same line, go to that line's neighborhood instead. Two Blue Line riders can get off at Damen in [Wicker Park](/blog/chicago/wicker-park). Two Red Line riders can get off at Chicago or Grand in [River North](/blog/chicago/river-north).

## How late can you stay out and still take the train home?

On the Red or Blue Line, as late as you like. The CTA [runs both lines 24 hours a day](https://www.transitchicago.com/welcome/), every day.

The Brown, Green, Orange, Pink and Purple lines stop overnight. If either of you rides one of them, look up the last train on the CTA's [schedules page](https://www.transitchicago.com/schedules/) before you order a second round. Check the CTA's [alerts page](https://www.transitchicago.com/alerts/) on the day of the date, too.

![](damen-blue-line-station-house-wicker-park.jpg)

This is why the late picks here are in River North and Wicker Park. A place near Damen or the Chicago Red Line stop still has a train home at 2 a.m.

## Should you book ahead or walk in?

Book the dinner and leave the drink open. A booked table fixes the start of the date, and an open second stop lets the night end early or run on.

Check the day of the week before you book. On a Monday, two of the bars here are closed. On a Tuesday, the museum is.

## Common questions

### What is a good first date idea in Chicago?

A drink at a bar near a station both of you reach without a transfer. Either of you can head home after one round. A bar on the Red or Blue Line also leaves room to stay out late.

### Which neighborhood is best for a date night in Chicago?

It depends on where you both start. The Loop is one ride for most lines, the West Loop has restaurants within a few blocks of Morgan, and Wicker Park suits two Blue Line riders.

### Can you do a date night in Chicago without a car?

Yes. Every place in this guide is within about a 12-minute walk of an 'L' station, and the Red and Blue lines run all night.
