When you search for basketball courts near me in Boston, start with the public park courts a short walk from a T station. Peters Park in the South End has two lit courts near the Orange Line, and Sennott Park in Cambridge is near the Red Line. Your pickup group lives in Cambridge, Dorchester and the South End, and every week someone says the court is too far.

This post lists six outdoor courts and one indoor gym by the line that reaches them. Then it covers how many players to invite, what to do once it gets cold, and the ride home. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which basketball courts near me can the whole group reach on the T?

Pick the court on the line most of your players already ride, so nobody transfers twice.

:::places

We checked the hours and walk times on these cards on Google Maps in November 2026. The court counts, renovations and gym details come from the City of Boston, the City of Cambridge and Boston Magazine.

### Orange and Green lines

Peters Park and Titus Sparrow Park are about a 19-minute walk apart in the South End. If the first court is full, the group can walk to the second. Langone Park suits players who come in through North Station.

![](massachusetts-avenue-orange-line-platform-boston.jpg)

### Red Line

The Red Line reaches three picks. Corporal Burns and Sennott Park serve Cambridge, and Moakley Park serves Dorchester and the South Shore. The City of Cambridge lists every one of its courts on its [athletic courts page](https://www.cambridgema.gov/iwantto/findanathleticcourt).

![](sennott-park-lawn-cambridge.jpg)

## How many players should you invite?

Invite about 12 for a full-court game, so you still have 10 if two people drop out. Four or six players is enough for half court.

Peters Park has two courts, so a big turnout can run two games at once. Titus Sparrow has one. At a Cambridge court, the city's court rules ask players to limit play to one hour when others are waiting. So a group of 12 may need to share.

## Where do you play once it gets cold?

Move indoors to a city community center. The Boston Centers for Youth and Families (BCYF) [doesn't charge a fee](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-mattahunt) to join its centers, but each player creates an account in its registration system first.

[BCYF Blackstone](https://www.boston.gov/community-centers/bcyf-blackstone) lists a gymnasium and weekday hours of 1 p.m. to 9 p.m. Gym time for adults changes by season, so call the center before you invite anyone. For a city where public gyms are free for players under 25, see our list of [free indoor basketball courts in NYC](/blog/new-york/basketball-courts-near-me-free-indoor-nyc).

## How does everyone get home after a late game?

The Boston courts close at 11:30 p.m. and Sennott Park at 10 p.m., so the T is still running when you leave. The [MBTA subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) lists service until about 1 a.m. most nights and 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

![](jfk-umass-red-line-platforms-dorchester.jpg)

From Moakley Park, check which Red Line branch you need before you walk to the station. The Ashmont and Braintree trains split at JFK/UMass. If the group wants food or a game on TV after, see [where to watch the game in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-watch-the-game-boston).

## Common questions

### Are there free indoor basketball courts in Boston?

Yes. BCYF community centers with gyms don't charge a membership fee, but you need a BCYF account. Adult gym hours vary by center, so call first.

### Do Boston's outdoor courts have lights?

Peters Park has lights for evening games, which [Boston Magazine](https://www.bostonmagazine.com/?p=561681) points out. The city's 2024 renovation of [Titus Sparrow Park](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/project/titus-sparrow-park-improvements) added new lighting and updated its sport courts.

### Do you need to reserve a court for pickup basketball?

No. These are public courts, and pickup games play on a first come, first served basis. Cambridge gives city and school events priority on its courts, so a scheduled event can take a court.
