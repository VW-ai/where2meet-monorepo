The best basketball courts near me in Boston, for a pickup group that lives all over the city, are the outdoor park courts a short walk from a T station. LoPresti Park sits by the Blue Line, Jackson Square Playground by the Orange Line, and Sennott Park by the Red Line. Your group has players in Eastie, Jamaica Plain and Cambridge, and nobody wants to ride three lines to shoot around.

This post sorts eight outdoor courts in seven neighborhoods by the line that reaches them. Then it covers how to pick a line when the group is split, which courts work after dark, and the last train home. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## Which basketball courts near me can the whole group reach on the T?

Pick the court on the line most of your players already ride, so nobody makes two transfers to play.

We checked the hours on these cards on Google Maps in November 2026, and the walk times are Google Maps walking directions from each station. The court and lighting details come from the City of Boston, the City of Cambridge and the East Boston Times.

### Blue and Orange lines

East Boston has one easy pick, and the Orange Line has four, from Charlestown down to Jackson Square. The [East Boston Times](https://eastietimes.com/?p=9258) reported new basketball courts with lighting when LoPresti Park's rebuild was finished. The city's [Mission Hill Playground page](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/improvements-mission-hill-playground) lists sports courts in its 2022 renovation.

### Red Line

The Red Line reaches three picks. Corporal Burns and Sennott Park serve Cambridge, and Moakley Park serves Dorchester and the South Shore. The City of Cambridge lists its public courts on its [athletic courts page](https://www.cambridgema.gov/iwantto/findanathleticcourt).

![](sennott-park-lawn-cambridge.jpg)

## How do you pick a line when the group is split?

Count where each player boards, then choose a court on the line with the most riders. Then check that nobody else needs more than one transfer.

For example, a group of four Orange Line riders, three Red Line riders and three Blue Line riders could play at Langone Park. The Orange riders go straight to North Station. The Red riders change once at Park Street and the Blue riders once at Government Center, then take the Green Line to North Station. The next week, rotate to Moakley Park or LoPresti Park so the same people don't make the long trip every time.

The city is redesigning Moakley Park in phases, so check the [Moakley Park page](https://www.boston.gov/moakley) before you invite a crowd there.

## Which courts work for a game after dark?

The Boston parks on this list stay open until 11:30 p.m., and LoPresti Park has lights. Sennott Park closes at 10 p.m., so a Cambridge run starts earlier.

Cambridge's court rules ask players to limit play to one hour when others are waiting. The city also gives its own and school events priority. A group of 12 at Sennott Park or Corporal Burns may end up sharing the court.

## How does everyone get home after a late game?

Take the T. The [MBTA subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) lists typical service until 1 a.m., and 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Riders who need a connection should reach a downtown transfer station by about 12:35 a.m., or 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday.

![](jfk-umass-red-line-platforms-dorchester.jpg)

From Moakley Park, check which Red Line branch you need before you walk to Andrew. The Ashmont and Braintree trains split at JFK/UMass. Check the [MBTA subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) the day of the game, too. If the group wants a game on TV after playing, see [where to watch the game in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-watch-the-game-boston).

## Common questions

### Are Boston's outdoor basketball courts free?

Yes. These are public park courts, so nobody pays and nobody books. Pickup games run on a first come, first served basis.

### Can you play outdoors in Boston in winter?

The courts keep the same hours all year, but a snowstorm can leave one covered. Ask a player who lives nearby to look at it before you send the invite, and keep an indoor option in mind for the coldest weeks.
