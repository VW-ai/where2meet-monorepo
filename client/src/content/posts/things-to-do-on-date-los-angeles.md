The easiest things to do on a date in Los Angeles sit a short walk from a Metro Rail station, so one of you can ride while the other parks once. A rooftop bar downtown, a wine bar in Old Pasadena and a free telescope in Griffith Park all work that way. Maybe you live in Santa Monica, your date lives in Pasadena, and neither of you wants to spend the first hour on the 10 or the 110.

This guide picks seven places in six areas, then covers which lines meet where, parking, the last train home and booking. It is part of our guide to [Los Angeles](/blog/los-angeles).

## Which things to do on a date are near a Metro station?

Every pick here is within about an 11-minute walk of a Metro Rail station, except the observatory, which a city bus serves from the B Line. The list runs from an early evening outing to bars open until 2 a.m.

:::places

The hours, reservations, price levels and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of November 2026, with walks from the nearest station. The observatory's admission and bus details come from [Griffith Observatory's getting-here page](https://griffithobservatory.lacity.gov/visit/getting-here/).

[Downtown](/blog/los-angeles/downtown) has the most lines. 7th St/Metro Center serves the A, B, D and E lines, so a date there rarely needs a transfer.

## Where should you meet if you live across town from each other?

Find the station where both of your lines already stop. In Los Angeles that is often downtown, because four of the six rail lines run through it.

For example, a Santa Monica rider on the E Line and a Pasadena rider on the A Line both reach 7th St/Metro Center and Little Tokyo/Arts District without changing trains. One of you in North Hollywood and the other in [Koreatown](/blog/los-angeles/koreatown) can meet in [Hollywood](/blog/los-angeles/hollywood) instead. The B Line runs from North Hollywood to Hollywood/Highland, and a D Line rider changes to the B at Wilshire/Vermont.

![](musso-frank-grill-hollywood-boulevard-facade.jpg)

## What if one of you drives?

Park at a station with a lot and ride the last few stops. Metro's [parking page](https://www.metro.net/riding/parking/) lists the lots, which include Union Station, Universal City, North Hollywood and several A Line stations in Pasadena.

The observatory is the exception. Its closest parking is paid, so a driver can park there while the other person takes the bus up from Vermont/Sunset.

## How late can you stay out and still ride home?

Plan to leave a bar by midnight if either of you rides Metro. The last B and D Line trains leave Union Station around midnight, and the last A Line train south leaves at 12:36 a.m., as Metro's [timetables](https://www.metro.net/riding/schedules/) show.

Two of the bars here stay open until 2 a.m., well after the trains stop. Check [Metro's service alerts](https://alerts.metro.net/) on the day of the date, too, since late service can change for maintenance.

## Common questions

### What are free things to do on a date in Los Angeles?

The observatory is the easiest one. Admission to the building, the grounds and the public telescopes is free, though parking costs money and planetarium shows have tickets.

### Can you plan a date in Los Angeles without a car?

Yes. Six of the seven places here are a short walk from a Metro Rail station, and a DASH bus runs up to the observatory. Keep the last train in mind, since Metro Rail doesn't run all night.

### Which night of the week is best for a date in LA?

Thursday to Sunday gives you every option here. On a Monday, the observatory and Musso & Frank are closed, and on Tuesday and Wednesday so is Spire 73.
