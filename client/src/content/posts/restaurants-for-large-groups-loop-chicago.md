Restaurants for large groups in the Loop work when they take a reservation and sit close to the train each person rides home. All seven restaurants for large groups below book tables, and each is a short walk from an 'L' stop or a Metra station. You're the one organizing. Half the group leaves offices on LaSalle Street, a couple of friends come in from the suburbs, and someone wants to catch a show after.

This post lists the seven places, then covers which side of the Loop to pick, how late each one stays open, and dinner before a show. It's part of our guide to [the Loop](/blog/chicago/the-loop), in [Chicago](/blog/chicago).

## Which restaurants for large groups in the Loop take a reservation?

All seven do. Pick by price and by the street the group can reach most easily, from Dearborn and State on the north side to Wabash and Franklin on the south.

## Which side of the Loop suits the trains home?

Start with the people who have the longest ride. Metra riders care most, because their last trains leave on a timetable and each line uses one station.

- **Union Station and Ogilvie** sit just west of the river. Seville is about a 7-minute walk from Union Station, and Roanoke about 12 minutes from Ogilvie.
- **Millennium Station**, at Randolph Street and Michigan Avenue, serves the Metra Electric line. Giordano's is the closest pick.
- **LaSalle Street Station** serves the Rock Island line, at the south end of the Loop. Seville is about 8 minutes away on foot, and Exchequer about 12.
- **'L' riders** can reach any of the seven, since the Red Line runs under State Street and the Blue Line under Dearborn Street.

Check each line on [Metra's schedules page](https://metra.com/schedules) before you settle on a time, and look at the [CTA alerts page](https://www.transitchicago.com/alerts/) the day before.

## How late do Loop restaurants serve dinner?

Earlier than you might expect. The Loop is an office district, and two of these restaurants close in the afternoon on some days.

The Walnut Room closes at 7 p.m. at the latest, so it works only for an early meal. On Sundays, Seville closes at 3 p.m. and Petterino's at 8 p.m. For a dinner that runs past 10, The Gage and Giordano's are the safest choices. The Red and Blue lines run at all hours, as the [Red Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/redline/) says, but the elevated lines stop overnight.

## Where can a group eat before a show in the Loop?

Stay on the north side, near Randolph Street. Petterino's shares a block with the Goodman Theatre, and the Walnut Room is about a 2-minute walk from the Lake Red Line stop.

Book for at least two hours before curtain, since a big table takes longer to order and pay. Ask for separate checks when you book, not when the bill arrives.

## Common questions

### Which Loop restaurant is open latest for a group?

The Gage serves until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays and until 11 p.m. on other nights. Giordano's stays open until 11 p.m. every night except Sunday, when it closes at 10.

### Where can a big group eat in the Loop on a Sunday?

Giordano's and The Gage keep the longest Sunday hours. Exchequer and Roanoke close at 9 p.m., and Seville and the Walnut Room close at 3 p.m.

### Is there a place for deep-dish pizza with a group near Millennium Park?

Yes. Giordano's on Randolph Street takes reservations and is about a 2-minute walk from Millennium Station, at the north end of the park.
