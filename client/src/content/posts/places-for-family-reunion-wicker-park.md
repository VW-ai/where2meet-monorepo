The best places for a family reunion in Wicker Park sit within about a 10-minute walk of the Damen Blue Line station. You can book a long table for dinner, let the kids loose in the park and walk relatives back to their beds without anyone needing a car. Maybe cousins are flying in from three states, your parents drive in from the suburbs, and you live a few blocks away. You need a weekend that works for a toddler and a grandmother at once.

This guide covers eight picks around Damen, the train in from O'Hare, which meals to book, and what to do between them.

## Where should a family reunion in Wicker Park happen?

Keep it on the blocks around the six-way corner of North, Damen and Milwaukee avenues. Every pick below is close enough to the Blue Line tracks that a relative who gets lost can follow them back to the group.

The hours, reservations, group fit, price levels and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of November 2026. Walk times start at the Damen Blue Line station unless a note says otherwise.

## How do relatives get here from O'Hare?

Put them on the Blue Line. It runs from O'Hare to Damen with no transfer, and Google Maps times the ride at about 32 minutes. O'Hare is the end of the line, so any train leaving the airport goes the right way.

- The [Blue Line](https://www.transitchicago.com/blueline/) runs at all hours, every day, so a late flight still has a train.
- Most picks are closest to [Damen](https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/rail-station/?StopId=50). For the inn, Damen and Division are about equal.
- Relatives staying downtown ride the same line the other way.
- Check [CTA alerts](https://www.transitchicago.com/alerts/) the day before for Blue Line changes or elevator outages, especially if someone uses a wheelchair or you're bringing a stroller.

## Which meals should you book, and which can you walk into?

Book the one meal where everyone sits together, and keep the rest loose. Big Star, Piece, Club Lucky and Lone Owl take reservations. Bongo Room doesn't, so leave it for whoever is up early.

Here is an example weekend for about 20 people:

1. **Friday evening.** Arrivals settle in, and whoever is free walks over to Big Star.
2. **Saturday lunch.** The whole family at Piece, booked for the full head count.
3. **Saturday afternoon.** The park, then the 606.
4. **Saturday dinner.** A smaller table at Club Lucky for the grandparents and anyone who wants a later night.
5. **Sunday morning.** Brunch at Lone Owl, then the Blue Line back to O'Hare.

When you call to book, give an exact head count and ask how many high chairs the restaurant has.

## What can kids and grandparents do between meals?

Use the park and the trail, which cost nothing. The 606 is an elevated trail on an old rail line, so it keeps kids on bikes away from street traffic. Grandparents who would rather sit can stay on the benches by the fountain while the kids play.

![](bloomingdale-trail-bikes-606-chicago.jpg)

## Common questions

### Can you get from O'Hare to Wicker Park without a car?

Yes. The Blue Line runs from O'Hare to Damen with no transfer, in about 32 minutes, at any hour.

### Is Wicker Park good for a reunion with young kids?

It can be. The park has a playground, and Piece and Big Star open by late morning. Plan the main meal for lunch or an early dinner so the youngest aren't up late.

### Where can relatives stay in Wicker Park?

The Wicker Park Inn is a bed and breakfast with rooms and apartments. If you need more rooms, book downtown and have relatives ride the Blue Line to Damen.
