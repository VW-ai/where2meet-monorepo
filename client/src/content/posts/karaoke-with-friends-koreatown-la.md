Karaoke with friends in Koreatown is easiest to plan around the Wilshire/Normandie station, where five of the seven picks below are within a 7-minute walk. Most open at 7 or 8 p.m. and stay open until 2 a.m., so a weekend night can start after dinner and run late. Your group wants to sing, but one friend lives in the Valley, two are on the Westside, and someone always drives.

This guide matches the picks to your group, covers booking a room on a Friday or Saturday, and explains how everyone gets home after midnight. It's part of our guide to [Koreatown](/blog/los-angeles/koreatown).

## Where can you do karaoke with friends in Koreatown?

Start on Wilshire Boulevard near Normandie Avenue, or a block north on 6th Street. Every pick below is open on Friday and Saturday nights.

## Private room or karaoke bar?

Book a private room when the group wants to pick every song and hold the mic all night. Choose a bar when the group is loose and people will come and go.

- **A set group that wants the night to itself.** Rosen and Feel both rent private rooms and take reservations.
- **Dinner first, without moving.** The Venue has a restaurant and karaoke rooms in one basement on Wilshire.
- **A group that grows through the night.** Cafe Brass Monkey is a bar, so a friend who shows up at 11 p.m. just walks in and finds the table.

## Should you book a room ahead on a weekend?

Yes, on Friday and Saturday. Six of the seven picks take reservations. Gaam doesn't list them, so plan to walk in there.

Settle the headcount before anyone calls. One person makes the booking with the final number, so the place can hold a room that fits. If two friends drop out, it's better to say so early than to pay for an empty seat. Give the latecomers the room's name or number in the group chat, because entrances on the upper floors and in the basements can be hard to spot from the street.

## How does everyone get there and home?

The D Line stops at Wilshire/Vermont, Wilshire/Normandie and Wilshire/Western. The B Line shares Wilshire/Vermont, so friends from Hollywood and North Hollywood ride in without changing trains.

The last D Line trains leave Koreatown a little after midnight, well before the rooms close. Look up the last train before the group books a late slot, using Metro's [schedules page](https://www.metro.net/riding/schedules/). Line 20 runs all night along Wilshire Boulevard with a stop at Wilshire/Vermont, and the [Koreatown page](/blog/los-angeles/koreatown) has its details. Metro lists no station parking in Koreatown, so drivers should use the valet at Feel or Gaam, the garage at The Venue, or street parking.

## Common questions

### How late are Koreatown karaoke spots open?

Most of these picks stay open until 2 a.m. Feel closes at 1:30 a.m., and The Venue closes at midnight on Sunday.

### Can you take the train home after karaoke?

Only if the group leaves early. The last D Line trains leave a little after midnight, so a 2 a.m. finish means Line 20 on Wilshire, a rideshare or a designated driver.

### Is there parking for karaoke in Koreatown?

The Metro stations here have no parking lots. Feel and Gaam offer valet, The Venue has a paid garage, and Rosen and Cafe Brass Monkey have street parking nearby.

### Is Koreatown in Los Angeles good for a weekend night out?

Yes, for a group that likes a late start. It sits between Downtown LA and Hollywood on two Metro lines, as the [Los Angeles guide](/blog/los-angeles) shows, and most picks here run until 2 a.m.
