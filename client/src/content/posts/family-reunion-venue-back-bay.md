A good family reunion venue in Back Bay seats your whole headcount and sits a short, step-free walk from a T station. Start with how many relatives are coming, because that number rules out most of the seven places below. Your headcount keeps changing, someone is pushing a stroller, one grandparent can't manage stairs, and relatives are arriving from three directions.

This post lists seven places, then covers room sizes, where relatives can stay, and which T stop works for strollers and wheelchairs. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay). For a Seaport lawn, a museum and other picks across town, see [where to have a family reunion in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-have-family-reunion-boston).

## Which family reunion venue should you pick in Back Bay?

Pick by headcount first, then by who's coming.

## How many relatives can each room hold?

Count seats, not standing room, because children and grandparents will need chairs. These are the sit-down numbers each venue publishes:

- **Up to 60.** [Rochambeau's](https://rochambeauboston.com/private-dining) Left Bank and Right Bank rooms seat 30 and 40. [The Tower's](https://longwoodvenues.com/venues/boston-historic-event-venue/) Green Room seats 50. The [College Club](https://thecollegeclubofboston.com/weddings-events/) seats up to 60.
- **60 to 100.** [Back Bay Social's](https://www.backbaysocial.com/private-events) Down Bar seats 65. Rochambeau's Bordeaux Room seats 75, and its Main Dining Room seats 90. The Copley Plaza's State Suite B seats 90.
- **100 to 200.** The Tower's Lounge, Library and Parlor seat 130, 140 and 180. The [Westin's](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/boswi-the-westin-copley-place-boston/events/) Essex North and Essex Center seat 150 each. The [Copley Plaza's](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/boston/fairmont-copley-plaza/social-events.html) State Suites seat 180.
- **Over 200.** The Westin's Staffordshire room seats 260, and its America Ballroom seats 1,200. The Copley Plaza's Grand Ballroom seats 580.

None of these event pages names a family reunion, so tell them that's your event. Ask about high chairs, ramps and any minimum spend.

## Where can out-of-town relatives stay?

Three of the seven have beds, so relatives can sleep close to the meal. The two hotels are about a 2-minute walk apart, which helps if one fills up.

From the Copley Plaza hotel, Parish Cafe is about an 8-minute walk. The Tower and Back Bay Social are each about 9 minutes, and Rochambeau about 12. Ask each hotel's sales office to hold a block of rooms, and when unbooked rooms go back.

The College Club's [bed and breakfast page](https://thecollegeclubofboston.com/bed-breakfast/) lists six double rooms and six singles. A double sleeps up to four adults. Ask the office about children, since the page doesn't mention them.

## Which T stop works for strollers and wheelchairs?

Use Copley, Arlington, Prudential or Back Bay station, which the MBTA lists as accessible. Avoid [Hynes](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-hymnl), which has no elevators.

That matters most for Rochambeau and Back Bay Social, the two picks closest to Hynes. A B, C or D rider with a stroller should get off at [Copley](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-coecl), about 10 minutes on foot from Rochambeau. An E rider can use [Prudential](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-prmnl), about 9 minutes from Back Bay Social.

Escalators at Copley and [Arlington](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-armnl) run up only, so plan on the elevator for the trip home. Check the MBTA's [elevator alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/access) and [subway alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/subway) the week of the reunion, since Green Line work can replace trains with shuttle buses.

## Common questions

### What is a good family reunion venue in Back Bay with young kids?

Back Bay Social and Parish Cafe both have children's menus. The Copley Plaza and the Westin are both listed as good for children.

### Can a family reunion be a brunch in Back Bay?

Yes. [Back Bay Social](https://www.backbaysocial.com/) serves brunch on Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. [Rochambeau](https://rochambeauboston.com/) serves it from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. every day, but it suits a mostly adult group.

### Where can relatives who drive park?

Back Bay Social, Rochambeau and Parish Cafe all list paid garage parking nearby. Ask the hotels about overnight parking.
