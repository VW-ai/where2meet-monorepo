If you're deciding where to host a networking event in Back Bay, book Workbar's event space by Arlington for a night with a speaker, or reserve a bar section near Copley for a mixer. Both stations are on all four Green Line branches, so guests from across the city arrive on one train. You run a professional group or a startup meetup, people RSVP from Cambridge, Brookline and the South End, and you need a room that matches the format.

This post suggests eight places, then covers which station to name in the invite and when to start. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay). For downtown and Fort Point options, see [networking event venue ideas in Boston](/blog/boston/networking-event-venue-boston).

## Where to host a networking event in Back Bay?

Pick by format first. A talk or a pitch night needs a bookable room, a mixer needs a bar that takes reservations, and a small group can meet over breakfast or coffee.

## How big a room does your meetup need?

Count RSVPs, then plan for fewer people to show. Book for the crowd you expect, not the list.

- **Under 20.** A reserved table or bar section is enough. Ask each bar how many guests a held section fits.
- **20 to 100.** Workbar says its rooms run from about 20 to 100 guests, for mixers, panels and pitch contests.
- **Several hundred.** Cvent lists about 13,000 square feet of meeting space at the Back Bay Events Center, with its largest listed room holding up to 500.

## Which T stop should the invite name?

Name Copley for anything near Copley Square, and Arlington for Workbar. Every Green Line branch stops at both.

Five of the eight places are closest to [Copley](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-coecl). Guests on the Orange Line or commuter rail can use [Back Bay station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta), which is about a 2-minute walk from OAK and about 4 from the Back Bay Events Center. Back Bay Social sits farther west on Boylston Street, so B, C and D riders should get off at Hynes.

![](berkeley-building-boylston-street-back-bay.jpg)

## When should a Back Bay networking event start?

Start an evening event at 6 p.m. on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, and end by 9 p.m. A breakfast meetup can start at 7:30 a.m. and finish before the workday.

Workbar takes evening events only after 5 p.m. on weekdays, so 6 p.m. leaves time to set up. For a mixer that runs late, OAK and Back Bay Social stay open well past 9 p.m. The MBTA's [subway guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/subway-guide) says to reach a downtown transfer station by 12:35 a.m. Sunday to Thursday, which leaves plenty of time.

## Common questions

### Can a meetup group book Workbar Back Bay without being members?

Yes. Workbar's event page says you don't need a membership to rent its event spaces. Rates start at $250 an hour and change by size, location and day.

### Where can a networking group meet in Back Bay on a weekend?

Workbar takes events anytime on weekends. Back Bay Social and OAK are open every day, and the Map Room Lounge is open on Saturday and Sunday afternoons.

### Is there a free place to host a networking event in Back Bay?

None of these places lists a free room. Capital One Café takes no reservations, so a small group can walk in and buy coffee instead of booking space.
