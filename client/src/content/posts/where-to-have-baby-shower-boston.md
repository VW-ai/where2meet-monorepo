If you're working out where to have a baby shower in Boston, start with the guest list. A short list fits a reserved table at a tea room or a brunch restaurant near a T stop, and a long one needs an event room you book by date. The parent-to-be wants a short trip, grandparents are coming in on the commuter rail, and half the friends live in Somerville.

This post lists eight places, then covers tea or brunch, when a private room is worth it, and how to keep the guest of honor's trip short. It's part of our guide to [Boston](/blog/boston).

## How do you choose where to have a baby shower in Boston?

Count the guests, then pick the format. Three places on this list are tables you reserve, and five are rooms you book by date.

:::places

The hours, reservations, price levels and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of October 2026. Each walk starts at the MBTA station the note names.

## Should the shower be afternoon tea or brunch?

Pick tea for a shower that starts after lunch, and brunch for one that starts in the morning. The hours decide it more than the menu does.

Grana stops serving at 2 p.m., so a brunch shower there should start by 11 a.m. That leaves time for food, a game and opening gifts. Both tea rooms run into the late afternoon, so a 1 p.m. or 2 p.m. start works there.

Watch the closed days. Silver Dove is closed Monday and Tuesday, and the Courtyard Tea Room is closed Monday. For a weekend shower, book the table as soon as the date is set.

## When does a baby shower need a private room?

Book a room once the guests won't fit at a couple of tables, or when the host wants games, a gift table and decorations.

Before you sign, ask each venue four things:

1. How many guests the room holds for a seated meal.
2. Whether you can bring your own decorations and cake.
3. What the minimum spend or rental fee is.
4. How early you can get in to set up.

Loft on Two is the one weekday pick. It suits coworkers who want to throw the shower over lunch without leaving the Financial District.

## Which T stop is easiest for the parent-to-be?

Start from the guest of honor's home and work outward. Their trip matters more than anyone else's, especially late in pregnancy.

- **Short walks.** The State Room, Silver Dove, the Courtyard Tea Room and Loft on Two are each about 2 minutes from a station.
- **Commuter rail guests.** Trains stop at South Station, Back Bay and North Station, so the downtown picks save relatives a transfer.
- **Somerville and Cambridge.** The Green Line D branch runs to Union Square, near Upstairs at Bow.

Check the MBTA's [elevator and escalator alerts](https://www.mbta.com/alerts/access) on the morning of the shower if anyone needs to avoid stairs.

## Common questions

### How far ahead should you book a baby shower venue in Boston?

Book as soon as you know the date. The event rooms on this list book by date, so the first host to ask gets the Saturday.

### What is a good baby shower venue in Boston for a small group?

A reserved table at Silver Dove or the Courtyard Tea Room works well for a small group. Both take reservations and stay open until at least 5 p.m. from Wednesday to Sunday.

### Can you have a baby shower on a weekday in Boston?

Yes. Grana serves every day until 2 p.m., and Loft on Two is open only on weekdays, so either fits a weekday lunch shower.
