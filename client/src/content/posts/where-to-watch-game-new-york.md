Where to watch the game in New York comes down to a sports bar on top of a station that everyone can reach without a transfer. Book a table for a big game, and ask the bar to put your match on before you send the plan. Your friends root for the same team, but they live in Brooklyn, Queens and New Jersey, and kickoff is at 10 a.m. on a Saturday.

This post suggests six bars in Manhattan, from Herald Square to the Financial District. Then it covers booking, early and late games, and getting home. It is part of our guide to [New York](/blog/new-york).

## Where to watch the game in New York with a group?

Pick a bar near a station where several lines stop. Each of these six is within a 5-minute walk of a subway entrance.

:::places

The hours, reservations, group sizes and walk times on these cards come from Google Maps as of October 2026. All six are listed there as good for watching sports.

Herald Square is the easiest hub for a mixed group. The B, D, F, M, N, Q, R and W stop at 34 St-Herald Sq, and PATH trains from Hoboken and Jersey City stop at [33 St](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/schedules-maps.html), one block away.

![](herald-square-clock-west-35th-street.jpg)

East Houston Street works for friends from Brooklyn and Queens on the F. Lower Manhattan works for friends on the Staten Island Ferry, which docks at Whitehall Street.

## Should you book a table for the game?

Book for a playoff game, a derby or any group bigger than four. Walk in only for a regular-season game with two or three friends.

When you call, ask three things:

- which screen will show your game, and whether the sound will be on
- whether a big game has a minimum spend or a cover charge
- how long the bar holds the table if someone is late

Arrive before the start, so the group sits together.

## What about early and late games?

Match the bar's hours to the start time. Soccer from Europe often starts in the New York morning, and games on the West Coast can end after midnight.

For a morning game, check the opening time on each card against kickoff. Not every bar opens before noon.

![](b-train-broadway-lafayette-station.jpg)

For a late game, check the trains home. Late at night, these lines stop at the bars' stations:

- 34 St-Herald Sq has the D, F, N and Q. The B, M, R and W don't run.
- Broadway-Lafayette St has the D and F, and 2 Av and 23 St on Sixth Avenue have the F.
- Whitehall St has the N and R, and the W doesn't run.
- 50 St on Eighth Avenue has the A and E, and the C doesn't run.

The MTA's [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) shows every line that runs overnight. If the group wants to play before it watches, see [where to play pickup basketball in New York](/blog/new-york/pickup-basketball-new-york).

## Common questions

### Where can I watch soccer early in the morning in New York?

Look for a sports bar that opens before kickoff on weekends, and book a table. Three of the bars here open by 9 a.m. on Saturdays.

### Do sports bars in New York take reservations for big games?

Many do. Five of the six bars here take reservations, so book as soon as the game time is set.

### Where should fans from New Jersey watch the game?

Try Herald Square. PATH trains from Hoboken and Jersey City stop at 33 St, a block from the 34 St-Herald Sq station. For a birthday that falls on game day, see [where to celebrate a birthday in New York](/blog/new-york/where-to-celebrate-birthday-new-york).
