If you're working out where to go on a date near Union Square, pick a place within a few blocks of the 14 St-Union Sq station. Eight subway lines stop there, so you both have a good chance of a direct ride there and home. Maybe one of you lives in Williamsburg on the L and the other works in Midtown near the 4, 5 and 6. Union Square is on both lines.

This post lists eight places, then covers the trip home and a backup plan. It is part of our guide to [Union Square](/blog/new-york/union-square).

## Where to go on a date near Union Square?

Choose by the kind of date, from a first coffee to a booked dinner. Walks are from the Union Square station, at 14th Street and Broadway, unless a card names another.

## How do you get home after a late date?

Check the trip home for the hour you'll leave, not the hour you arrive. Fewer trains stop here late at night.

Late at night, the 5, R and W don't stop at Union Square, but the 4, 6, L, N and Q still do. On weekends, the W doesn't run. The MTA's [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) shows what runs from about 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.

## What if the first place doesn't work out?

Keep a second place a short walk away. If the wait is long or the room is too loud to talk, you can move without getting back on a train.

The [Union Square Greenmarket](https://grownyc.org/locations/union-square-greenmarket/) runs on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. For a bigger group, see our [group dinner spots in Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan/how-to-pick-a-restaurant-for-a-group-dinner). ## Common questions

### Is Union Square a convenient place for a first date?

It is when you come from different directions, because eight lines stop at 14 St-Union Sq. The L runs straight here from Williamsburg and Bushwick.

### Which trains still stop at Union Square late at night?

The 4, 6, L, N and Q. The 5, R and W don't stop here late at night, so check the [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) before you pick a closing time.

### Where exactly should we meet?

Name the plaza at the south end of the park, along 14th Street. On Greenmarket days, add a corner, since the market fills the north and west sides.
