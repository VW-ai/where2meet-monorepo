Group activities in SoMa cover ping pong, bowling, arcade games, pinball and virtual reality, at seven spots within a 10-minute walk of a Muni, BART or Caltrain station. Most open in the afternoon on weekends and run into the evening, so one plan can hold a game, food and drinks. Your friends want to do something on Saturday besides sit at a table, and they're coming from the Peninsula, the East Bay and the far side of the city.

This guide sorts the picks by the station they're close to, flags which ones close on Sunday, and covers getting home after a late game. It's part of our guide to [SoMa](/blog/san-francisco/soma).

## Where can you find group activities in SoMa?

Look at three clusters. The north end sits around Yerba Buena Gardens and Market Street, the south end around 4th and King, and Western SoMa near 11th and Folsom Streets.

## Which end of SoMa should the group start from?

Start where most of the group's trips end. Friends on BART or the Muni Metro lines under Market Street reach the north end fastest. Friends on Caltrain reach the south end on foot.

- **North end.** Powell St serves BART and the Muni Metro. The Yerba Buena/Moscone station on 4th Street serves the T Third. SPIN, Sandbox VR and Yerba Buena Bowling Center are each about a 5-minute walk or less from one of the two.
- **South end.** The Caltrain station at 4th and King also has T Third and N Judah stops. Lucky Strike and Thriller Social Club are both under a 7-minute walk away.
- **Western SoMa.** BuzzWorks and The Willows are about a 10-minute walk from the Van Ness Muni station on Market Street.

SPIN and Lucky Strike are about a 13-minute walk apart, or a few stops on the T Third, so a group can play at one end and eat at the other.

## What's open on Saturday and what's open on Sunday?

Saturday has the most choice, since all seven picks are open. On Sunday, SPIN and Thriller Social Club are closed, so pick from the other five.

For a Sunday plan, Lucky Strike opens at 11 a.m. and Yerba Buena Bowling Center at noon. BuzzWorks opens Sunday at 10 a.m. but is closed Monday through Thursday. A Friday night works too, since Thriller Social Club and BuzzWorks both stay open until at least 1:30 a.m.

## How does everyone get home after a late game?

Check the last train before you book a late slot. BART closes around midnight, as its [schedules page](https://www.bart.gov/schedules) says. Caltrain riders should look up the last weekend train from Caltrain's [San Francisco station page](https://www.caltrain.com/station/sanfrancisco) before the night starts.

The T Third runs until midnight on weekends. After that, Muni's [Owl buses](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/routes-stops/muni-owl-service-late-night-transportation) run every half hour overnight, including the 91 Owl on 3rd and 4th Streets.

## Common questions

### What are good group activities in SoMa on a rainy day?

Every pick here is indoors, so bowling, ping pong, pinball and VR all work in the rain. For bowling, choose Lucky Strike or Yerba Buena Bowling Center.

### Do you need to book ahead for bowling or ping pong in SoMa?

Lucky Strike, SPIN and Thriller Social Club all take reservations. Book ahead on a Saturday when the group has more than a few people.

### Which SoMa activities are open on Sunday?

Lucky Strike, Yerba Buena Bowling Center, Sandbox VR, BuzzWorks and The Willows are open on Sunday. SPIN and Thriller Social Club are closed that day.

### Is SoMa a good part of San Francisco for a weekend with friends?

Yes, for a group spread across the Bay Area. SoMa has Caltrain, BART and several Muni lines, as the [San Francisco guide](/blog/san-francisco) shows, so friends from the Peninsula and the East Bay can each ride in without a car.
