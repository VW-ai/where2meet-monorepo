Where to celebrate graduation in Lower Manhattan depends on who's coming and when the ceremony ends. Book a family meal near the Franklin St, Fulton St or World Trade Center stations, then pick a rooftop for the graduates later. Your parents are flying in, your grandparents can't walk far, your friends want to go out after, and the family chat wants one plan.

This post suggests eight places, from a Tribeca lunch to a rooftop bar near Wall Street. Then it covers booking in graduation season, getting the family downtown and splitting the day between family and friends. It is part of our guide to [Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan).

## Where to celebrate graduation after a downtown ceremony?

Pick a place a few minutes' walk from a station on the line your family rides. Tribeca suits a sit-down meal, and the Financial District suits drinks afterward.

Two colleges have their main buildings downtown. Pace University is at One Pace Plaza, across from City Hall Park. The Borough of Manhattan Community College is on Chambers Street in Tribeca, the same street as the Chambers St station.

![](borough-of-manhattan-community-college-chambers-street.jpg)

## Should you book a graduation meal ahead?

Yes. Book as soon as you know the ceremony date, because other families from the same class want a table the same afternoon.

Leave time between the ceremony and the meal. Ceremonies can run long, and photos after take longer. A table about two hours after the scheduled end gives everyone time to get there.

When you book, ask the restaurant:

- whether it has a private room or one long table for your group
- whether a large group pays a deposit or a minimum spend
- whether it has high chairs, if young kids are coming
- how long you can keep the table

## How does the family get downtown?

Pick a place right next to a station when older relatives are coming. Paros and Osteria Carlina are each about a 1-minute walk from Franklin St.

Families from New Jersey can take PATH to [World Trade Center](https://www.panynj.gov/path/en/stations/world-trade-center-station.html), where trains from Newark arrive at all hours. Family from Staten Island can take the [Staten Island Ferry](https://siferry.com/), which is free and runs 24 hours a day to Whitehall Street. Fulton St connects the 2, 3, 4, 5, A, C and J in one station.

![](peck-slip-south-street-seaport.jpg)

The Seaport has older streets paved with cobblestones. Leave extra time there for anyone with a cane, a stroller or a wheelchair.

## How do you celebrate with family and friends on the same day?

Plan two stops. Have lunch or an early dinner with family, then move to a bar with friends.

The rooftop bars open at 4 p.m. or 5 p.m., so a noon lunch and evening drinks fit in one day. For more on booking a big table, see our [group dinner spots in Lower Manhattan](/blog/new-york/lower-manhattan/how-to-pick-a-restaurant-for-a-group-dinner). If the day doubles as a birthday, see [where to celebrate a birthday in New York](/blog/new-york/where-to-celebrate-birthday-new-york).

Check the trains home before the night ends. Late at night, the 3, 5 and C don't run here, and the 2, 4, A, E, J and 1 run all night. The MTA's [late-night subway map](https://www.mta.info/map/5336) shows every line that runs overnight.

## Common questions

### Where can a big family celebrate graduation in Lower Manhattan?

Choose a restaurant with a private dining room, and book it early. Locanda Verde and Skinos both have one, and both open before noon.

### Can you rent a graduation party venue in Lower Manhattan?

Yes. Ask a restaurant or bar with a private room about a minimum spend for the group. Highwater Rooftop has a private room for a party with friends.

### What's a good place for graduation brunch downtown?

Try a restaurant that opens early. Locanda Verde opens at 7 a.m. and serves brunch, and Paros and Skinos open at 11:30 a.m.
