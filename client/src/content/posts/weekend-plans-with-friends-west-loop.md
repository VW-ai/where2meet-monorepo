Good weekend plans with friends in the West Loop start near the Morgan station, where coffee, bowling, arcade games and an all-day restaurant sit within a 10-minute walk. The Green and Pink lines stop there, and Metra riders are a short walk east, which makes it a convenient base for a group coming from all over. You want a weekend day that is more than one dinner. Maybe some friends live on the North Side, one lives in Pilsen, and someone is coming in from the suburbs.

This post picks a home base, matches the games spots to your group, covers the friends on Metra and says what changes on a Sunday. It's part of our guide to the [West Loop](/blog/chicago/west-loop).

## What are good weekend plans with friends in the West Loop?

Pick one games spot near Green Street and Fulton Market, then add coffee or a park before it and a meal after. Every pick below is open on Saturday.

## Which games spot suits your group?

Book ahead for a set group, and walk in for a loose one. The three games picks differ mostly in how much planning they need.

- **A fixed headcount.** Punch Bowl Social takes reservations, so a group of eight can book a set time instead of waiting for space.
- **People arriving at different times.** Emporium opens at noon on weekends and runs late, so early arrivals can play while the rest are still on the train.
- **A slower afternoon.** Recess suits a group that would rather sit for three hours than move around.

Punch Bowl Social, Emporium and Beatrix are within about two blocks of each other around Green Street and Fulton Market. A group can switch plans there without anyone looking up a new route.

## What if some friends come in on Metra?

Meet them at the Chicago French Market, then walk west together. Union Pacific North, Northwest and West trains stop at Ogilvie, right beside it.

Friends on the Milwaukee District or BNSF lines arrive at Union Station instead, about a 6-minute walk from the market. From the market, Punch Bowl Social is about a 14-minute walk west. Weekend trains run on different timetables than weekday ones, so check the Saturday or Sunday schedule on [Metra's schedules page](https://metra.com/schedules) before you set a start time. Look up the last train home before you plan the evening, not after.

## Does the plan change on a Sunday?

Yes. The Chicago French Market is closed on Sunday, and two of the meal picks close earlier.

- Meet Metra riders at Sawada Coffee instead. It opens at 8 a.m. on Sunday and is about a 10-minute walk from Ogilvie.
- Recess closes at 10 p.m. on Sunday and Beatrix at 9 p.m., so move dinner earlier.
- Emporium stays open until 2 a.m. on Sunday night if the group wants a late finish.

For the ride home, the Blue Line runs all night from Clinton, next to Union Station. The Green and Pink lines at Morgan stop for a few hours overnight, as the [Green Line page](https://www.transitchicago.com/greenline/) shows. Check [CTA alerts](https://www.transitchicago.com/alerts/) the day before for weekend track work.

## Common questions

### Is the West Loop good for a weekend with friends?

Yes, for a group that wants food and games close together. Most picks here are within a 10-minute walk of the Morgan station, so the group can change plans on foot.

### Do we need reservations on a Saturday?

Punch Bowl Social, Recess and Beatrix all take reservations, so book ahead for six or more. The park and the coffee shop are the easy stops that need no plan.

### How late can we stay out in the West Loop?

Emporium is open until 3 a.m. on Saturday night. After the Green and Pink lines stop, take the Blue Line from Clinton or a rideshare home.
