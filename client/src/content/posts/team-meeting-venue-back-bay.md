A team meeting venue in Back Bay lists from $39 to $105 an hour for up to 10 people, and from $76 to $200 an hour for 12. Headcount moves the price more than the street does, so count chairs before you compare rooms. Your team works in or near Back Bay, it needs a room with a door for a few hours, and you have a budget to stay inside.

This post lists six rooms, prices them by headcount and explains how to book each one. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay).

## Which team meeting venue in Back Bay fits your headcount?

Start with the number of chairs. For a small team, WeWork and Workbar by Arlington and Copley list the lowest rates. At 10 to 12, 177 Huntington and Spaces cost less than Workbar, and past 20 only the hotel has room.

## What does a Back Bay meeting room cost by the hour?

The price follows the headcount. Every rate here is a listed price before tax, so confirm it when you book.

- **2 to 4 people.** WeWork's huddle rooms start at $39, Workbar charges $50, and the 4-seat room at 177 Huntington starts at $59.
- **5 to 10 people.** Workbar charges $75 for 5 to 7 and $100 for 8 to 10. The 10-seat room at 177 Huntington starts at $76, and Spaces' 8- and 10-seat rooms at $105 and $89.
- **11 to 14 people.** The 12-seat boardrooms start at $76 at 177 Huntington and $89 at Spaces. Idea Space charges $99, and Workbar charges $200 for 10 to 14.
- **15 to 20 people.** Workbar charges $250. WeWork lists boardrooms for 10 to 20 from $99, so check what 501 Boylston has in the app.
- **21 or more.** Workbar charges $300 an hour. Past that, ask the Hilton for a quote.

As an example, a three-hour planning session for 12 comes to $228 at the 177 Huntington boardroom's starting rate, $297 at Idea Space and $600 at Workbar.

For a longer session, compare day rates. Idea Space charges $349 for a half day and $599 for a full day, so its half day costs less than four single hours. Workbar's day rates start at $360, with 10 percent off a full day.

Rates come from [Workbar](https://www.workbar.com/meeting-room-rental), [WeWork](https://www.wework.com/solutions/meeting-rooms), Davinci's listings for [177 Huntington](https://www.davincimeetingrooms.com/loc/us/massachusetts/boston-meeting-rooms/6373) and [Spaces](https://www.davincimeetingrooms.com/loc/us/massachusetts/boston-meeting-rooms/4916), and [Idea Space](https://ideaspaceboston.com/reserve-space/). [Cvent](https://www-eur.cvent.com/venues/boston/hotel/hilton-boston-back-bay/venue-61d34498-dfb1-4523-9530-a86ba2ce7150) lists the Hilton's rooms.

## How do you book a Back Bay room without a membership?

Book online at the five coworking rooms and ask the Hilton for a quote. Workbar and WeWork both say anyone can book by the hour.

- **Workbar.** Reserve on its online calendar. Members save 25 percent.
- **WeWork.** Pick 501 Boylston and a time in the WeWork app, and pay by card.
- **Spaces and 177 Huntington.** Book through Davinci Meeting Rooms, online or by phone.
- **Idea Space.** Use the Book Now button on its reserve page.

The coworking rooms keep weekday hours, roughly 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. For an evening session, call Spaces about after-hours bookings. For outside guests after work, see [where to host a networking event in Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay/where-to-host-networking-event-back-bay).

## Common questions

### Where can we rent a meeting room by the hour in Back Bay?

Workbar, WeWork, Spaces, Idea Space and the rooms at 177 Huntington all rent by the hour on weekdays. Workbar and WeWork need no membership.

### What is the cheapest meeting room in Back Bay for 10 people?

At the listed starting rates, the 10-seat room at 177 Huntington costs least, from $76 an hour. Spaces' 10-seat room starts at $89, and Workbar charges $100 for 8 to 10.

### Can we book a Back Bay meeting room on a weekend?

The coworking rooms in this post keep weekday hours. Ask Spaces about after-hours bookings, or send the Hilton's events team your date.
