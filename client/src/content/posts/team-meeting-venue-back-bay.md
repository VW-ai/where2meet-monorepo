A good team meeting venue in Back Bay is a bookable room a short walk from the Green Line, sized to your headcount. Workbar by Arlington, Idea Space on Boylston Street and the Hilton on Dalton Street cover most teams. Your people ride different Green Line branches, someone comes in on the Orange Line, and you're the one who has to book the room.

This post suggests seven places, then covers which T stop suits each one and what the rooms cost. It's part of our guide to [Back Bay](/blog/boston/back-bay), and [where to meet for a team meeting in Boston](/blog/boston/where-to-meet-team-meeting-boston) covers the rest of the city.

## Which team meeting venue in Back Bay fits your group?

Decide by headcount and length. A working session needs a room, a full day or a big group needs the hotel, and a short check-in or a team lunch can happen at a table.

Two pairs share an address. You can get coffee at Tatte before a morning at Workbar, or go down to Back Bay Social after an afternoon at Idea Space. Neither plan moves the team across the neighborhood.

## Which T stop should your team use?

Count how many people ride each line, then pick the venue nearest most of them. Back Bay's stops serve different branches, so the right room depends on who's coming.

- **Any Green Line branch.** All four stop at [Copley](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-coecl) and Arlington, so Workbar suits a team spread across every branch.
- **B, C and D or E.** West of Copley the B, C and D stop at Hynes, and the E stops at Prudential. The Hilton sits between the two.
- **Orange Line and commuter rail.** [Back Bay station](https://www.mbta.com/stops/place-bbsta) has the Orange Line and the Framingham/Worcester, Franklin/Foxboro, Needham and Providence/Stoughton lines. Workbar and Idea Space are each about a 12-minute walk from it, and the Hilton about 15.

An E branch rider going to Idea Space or Terra should get off at Copley, about a 7-minute walk, because the E doesn't stop at Hynes.

## What does a meeting room in Back Bay cost?

Expect $50 to $100 an hour for up to 10 people at Workbar, and $99 an hour for the 12-seat room at Idea Space. A large event at the Hilton needs a quote.

[Workbar's meeting room page](https://www.workbar.com/meeting-room-rental) lists these hourly rates. It says prices vary by room size and location, so check the Back Bay calendar before you budget.

- 2 to 4 people, $50
- 5 to 7 people, $75
- 8 to 10 people, $100
- 10 to 14 people, $200
- 15 to 20 people, $250
- 21 or more people, $300

Daily rates start at $360. As an example, two hours for six people comes to $150 at the listed rate.

[LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/ma/boston/idea-space) lists the Idea Space room at $99 an hour, $375 a half day or $699 a day. Other booking sites show different prices, so confirm with Idea Space before you commit.

[Cvent](https://www-eur.cvent.com/venues/boston/hotel/hilton-boston-back-bay/venue-61d34498-dfb1-4523-9530-a86ba2ce7150) lists about 15,000 square feet of meeting space at the Hilton, for programs of up to 500 guests. Send the hotel your headcount and date, and ask for its smallest room that fits.

## Common questions

### Can we book a room in Back Bay without joining a coworking space?

Yes. Workbar's page says anyone can book its rooms by the hour or by the day with no membership. Members get 25 percent off.

### Where can a team meet in Back Bay after 5 p.m. or on a weekend?

Workbar and Idea Space close at 5 p.m. on weekdays and stay closed on weekends. Terra opens at 10:30 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday, and Back Bay Social is open until 2 a.m. every night. Ask the Hilton about its rooms for those times.

### Which meeting room in Back Bay is closest to the T?

Workbar, about a 1-minute walk from Arlington, where all four Green Line branches stop. Copley, also on all four branches, is about a 7-minute walk away.
