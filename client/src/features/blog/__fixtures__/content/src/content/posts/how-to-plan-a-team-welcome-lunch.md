_Sample copy for tests._

A team welcome lunch works best at a place the whole team can reach in one trip, close to the office and easy to book for a group. Pick it a week ahead so the new teammate knows the plan before their first day.

This post covers where to hold the lunch, how far ahead to book and how to settle the place with your team. The examples are in New York.

## Where should a team welcome lunch be?

Pick a place near the office or near a station most of the team already uses. In New York, Grand Central and Rockefeller Center put many subway lines within a short walk.

## How far ahead should you book a team welcome lunch?

Book at least a week ahead for a group of seven or more. Ask whether the place holds a table for a big group at lunchtime, and whether it splits the bill.

## How long should the lunch take?

Plan for about an hour. A set menu or counter service gets everyone back to work on time.

## Common questions

### Should the new teammate pick the place?

Ask them which food they like, and let the organizer handle the booking.

## A quick checklist

- Book a week ahead.
- Check the table fits the whole team.
- Send the address and the nearest station the day before.
