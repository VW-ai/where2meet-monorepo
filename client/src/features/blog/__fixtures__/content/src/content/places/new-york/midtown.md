_Sample copy for tests._

Midtown is where many New York offices are, so it's the default for anything work-related. Grand Central, Times Square and Herald Square stations put most of the city within one train, and Bryant Park sits between them as an easy landmark. Most of these posts are about weekday plans, like a team lunch, a quick meeting near the office or drinks after work, chosen so the whole group can arrive within a short walk of one station.

## Getting around

_Sample copy._ Grand Central, Times Square and Herald Square stations serve most subway lines, and PATH and commuter trains stop nearby. Streets are busiest from 8 to 10 a.m. and 5 to 7 p.m.
