_Sample copy for tests._

New York is easy to reach and hard to agree on. A group spread across Brooklyn, Queens and Manhattan can spend longer choosing a place than getting there. These posts collect places that work for common plans, chosen so most people can arrive on one subway line. Each post names a few real places and the stations near them, so you can compare options before you share the plan.

## Getting around

_Sample copy._ Most trips here are by **subway**. A place within a short walk of a station served by several lines, like Herald Square or Times Square, is usually the most convenient pick for a mixed group.

- Check weekend service changes before you send the plan.
- Allow extra time for crossing between boroughs at rush hour.
