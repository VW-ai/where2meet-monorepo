_Sample copy for tests._

The easiest group dinner spots in New York sit next to a station where many subway lines stop, like Herald Square. Everyone gets there on one train, and nobody faces a long trip home after dinner.

This post covers where to look around Herald Square, how to book for a big group and how to settle the place together.

## Why is Herald Square easy to reach?

The 34th St-Herald Sq station serves the B, D, F, M, N, Q, R and W, and PATH trains stop next door.

![](herald-square-plaza-new-york.jpg)

## Where can a big group eat near Herald Square?

Koreatown is a short walk east, with restaurants built for sharing.

:::places

## How late do trains run after dinner?

The subway runs all night, but trains come less often after midnight. Check the trip home before you book a late table.

![](times-square-crowds-new-york.jpg)
