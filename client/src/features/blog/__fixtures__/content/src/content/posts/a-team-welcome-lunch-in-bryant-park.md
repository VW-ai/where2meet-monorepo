A team welcome lunch in Bryant Park is easy to reach from most Midtown offices, and the kiosks let everyone buy their own food. The movable chairs fit a big group without a booking.

This post covers where to sit, what to do if it rains and how to settle the plan.

## Where should the team sit in Bryant Park?

The lawn and the terrace by the library have the most chairs.

![](midtown-view-from-empire-state-building.jpg)

## What if it rains?

Book a room nearby as a backup.

:::places

## How do people get to Bryant Park?

The B, D, F and M stop at the park's corner, and the 7 stops at Fifth Avenue.
